MitoTracker™ Dyes for Flow Cytometry & Imaging

biotech Invitrogen / Thermo Fisher Scientific • Catalog Numbers M46750, M46751, M46752, M46753

info Overview & Principle

MitoTracker™ Dyes for Flow Cytometry & Imaging (Thermo Fisher MAN0019253) are cell-permeant mitochondrion-selective fluorescent probes that passively diffuse across the plasma membrane and selectively accumulate within active mitochondria in live cells.

Probes & Mechanisms:

  • MitoTracker™ Green FM (Cat. M7514, Ex/Em 490/516 nm): Accumulates in mitochondria independent of mitochondrial membrane potential ($\Delta\Psi_m$). Useful for quantifying total mitochondrial mass. (Non-fixable).
  • MitoTracker™ Orange CMTMRos (Cat. M7510, Ex/Em 554/576 nm): Contains a chloromethyl thiol-reactive moiety. Membrane potential-dependent accumulation; retained after aldehyde fixation.
  • MitoTracker™ Red CMXRos (Cat. M7512, Ex/Em 579/599 nm): Membrane potential-dependent probe with chloromethyl group. Fluoresces in PE-Texas Red channel; retained after aldehyde fixation and permeabilization.
  • MitoTracker™ Deep Red FM (Cat. M22426, Ex/Em 644/665 nm): Far-red probe (APC channel); potential-dependent, fixable with formaldehyde.

format_list_numbered Usage Procedure

Live Cell Staining Protocol for Flow Cytometry & Imaging

1

Stock Solution Reconstitution (1 mM)

Warm lyophilized MitoTracker™ vial to room temperature. Reconstitute 1 vial (50 µg) in high-quality anhydrous DMSO to yield a 1 mM stock solution (e.g. add 74 µL DMSO to 50 µg MitoTracker Red CMXRos [MW 671.7], or 92 µL to Green FM [MW 671.9]). Vortex to dissolve.

infoAliquot stock solution into dark desiccated vials and store at -20°C.
schedule 5 mins
2

Prepare Working Staining Solution (20–100 nM)

Dilute 1 mM stock solution into prewarmed (37°C) complete culture medium or PBS to a working concentration of 20–100 nM (e.g. 1 µL of 1 mM stock into 10–50 mL medium).

schedule 2 mins
3

Staining Incubation

Aspirate culture medium from adherent cells or pellet suspension cells (200 × g 5 min). Add 100 nM working staining solution. Incubate at 37°C in 5% CO2 for 15–45 minutes in the dark.

schedule 15–45 mins
4

Live Cell Analysis

For live-cell flow cytometry or confocal microscopy, cells can be analyzed immediately in the staining solution or after replacing with fresh prewarmed medium/PBS.

schedule 10 mins

Aldehyde Fixation Protocol (CMXRos, CMTMRos, Deep Red)

1

Post-Staining Fixation (3.7% Formaldehyde)

Following 37°C incubation with fixable MitoTracker™ dyes (Red CMXRos, Orange CMTMRos, or Deep Red FM), aspirate staining solution. Add 3.7% formaldehyde in prewarmed PBS. Incubate at 37°C for 15 minutes.

infoMitoTracker Green FM is NOT fixable; do not use formaldehyde on Green FM.
schedule 15 mins
2

Washing

Rinse cells 3 times with PBS for 5 minutes each at room temperature.

schedule 15 mins
3

Permeabilization (Optional for Antibody Co-Staining)

Incubate fixed cells in 0.1% Triton X-100 in PBS for 10 minutes at room temperature. Wash with PBS before blocking and adding primary antibodies for immunofluorescence.

schedule 10 mins

Instrument Configuration & Gating Guide

1

Excitation & Emission Channel Selection

• Green FM: 488 nm laser -> FITC / FL1 (530/30 nm filter) • Orange CMTMRos: 561 nm or 488 nm laser -> PE / FL2 (585/42 nm filter) • Red CMXRos: 561 nm or 488 nm laser -> PE-Texas Red / FL3 (610/20 nm filter) • Deep Red FM: 633/640 nm laser -> APC / FL4 (660/20 nm filter)

schedule Setup
2

Mitochondrial Depolarization Control (CCCP / FCCP)

Pre-treat control cells with 50 µM CCCP (carbonyl cyanide m-chlorophenyl hydrazone) for 15 minutes at 37°C prior to MitoTracker Red CMXRos staining. Confirm collapse of mitochondrial membrane potential reflected by loss of fluorescence.

schedule 15 mins

calculate Mitochondrial Mass & Membrane Potential Metrics

Geometric Mean Fluorescence Intensity (MFI) = 10^[ (1/N) · Σ log10(FL_i) ] ΔΨm Depolarization Ratio = MFI_treated / MFI_control

1. Total Mass vs Potential: MitoTracker Green FM reflects total mitochondrial mass (independent of $\Delta\Psi_m$); MitoTracker Red CMXRos / Orange CMTMRos reflects energized mitochondrial membrane potential ($\Delta\Psi_m$).

2. Ratio Metric: Normalized potential = $ ext{MFI}_{ ext{CMXRos}} / ext{MFI}_{ ext{Green FM}}$.

Storage & Stability

Store desiccated at ≤-20°C protected from light. Reconstituted stock should be stored at -20°C.

event_available Stable for 6 months at -20°C protected from light.

warning Cautions & Safety

  • check_circle Reconstitute only in high-quality anhydrous DMSO; exposure to moisture degrades the chloromethyl reactive group.
  • check_circle Do not stain cells at 4°C; mitochondrial uptake requires active membrane transport at 37°C.
  • check_circle Excessive dye concentration (> 500 nM) can cause non-specific cytoplasmic background and mitochondrial uncoupling.
analytics

Expected Results

Healthy cells show high geometric MFI with sharp mitochondrial network localization; apoptotic or uncoupled cells (CCCP-treated) show > 80% decrease in Red CMXRos fluorescence.
