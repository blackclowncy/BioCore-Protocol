MitoTracker™ Dyes for Flow Cytometry & Imaging

biotech Invitrogen / Thermo Fisher Scientific • Catalog Numbers M46750, M46751, M46752, M46753

info Overview & Principle

MitoTracker™ Dyes are cell-permeant reagents that passively diffuse across the plasma membrane and selectively accumulate within active mitochondria in live cells driven by mitochondrial membrane potential (ΔΨm).

Features: Optimized concentrations for conventional and spectral flow cytometry as well as live-cell confocal imaging. Certain variants (e.g. MitoTracker Red CMXRos / Orange CMTMRos) feature chloromethyl reactive moieties that covalently bind mitochondrial proteins, permitting subsequent aldehyde fixation and permeabilization.

format_list_numbered Usage Procedure

Mitochondrial Labeling Protocol for Flow Cytometry

1

Reconstitute Stock Solution

Dissolve lyophilized dye in anhydrous DMSO to prepare a 1 mM stock solution. Aliquot and store at -20°C protected from light.

schedule 5 mins
2

Prepare Working Staining Medium

Dilute stock solution into pre-warmed culture medium or buffer to a working concentration of 20–100 nM.

schedule 5 mins
3

Incubate Live Cells

Harvest cells, resuspend in working dye solution, and incubate in 37°C incubator (5% CO2) for 15–30 minutes.

schedule 20 mins
4

Wash & Acquire Data

Centrifuge at 300 × g for 5 mins, resuspend in fresh pre-warmed buffer, and acquire on flow cytometer immediately.

schedule 10 mins

Optional Fixation Protocol (CMXRos / Fixable Dyes)

1

Live Staining First

Stain live cells with fixable MitoTracker probe for 20 mins at 37°C before any fixation.

schedule 20 mins
2

Fix with Formaldehyde

Fix with 3.7% formaldehyde in growth medium/PBS at 37°C for 15 minutes.

schedule 15 mins
3

Permeabilize & Co-stain

Permeabilize with 0.1% Triton X-100 in PBS for 5 mins for antibody co-staining.

schedule 10 mins

Storage & Stability

Store desiccated at ≤-20°C protected from light. Reconstituted stock should be stored at -20°C.

event_available Stable for 6 months at -20°C protected from light.

warning Cautions & Safety

  • check_circle Never fix cells before staining with MitoTracker dyes; mitochondrial accumulation depends strictly on active membrane potential.
  • check_circle High concentrations (>200 nM) can cause non-specific cellular staining and mitochondrial respiratory inhibition.
  • check_circle Dyes are light-sensitive; protect from ambient laboratory lighting during handling.
analytics

Expected Results

High fluorescence signal specific to mitochondrial network in cells with intact mitochondrial potential; depolarized/apoptotic cells display marked reduction in fluorescence.
