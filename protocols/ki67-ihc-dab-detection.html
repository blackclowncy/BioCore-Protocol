VitroView™ In Situ Ki67 IHC DAB Detection Kit

biotech VitroVivo Biotech • Catalog Number VB-4002D (50–100 tests)

info Overview & Principle

The VitroView™ In Situ Ki67 IHC DAB Detection Kit (Cat. No. VB-4002D) is optimized for one-step, biotin-free immunohistochemical detection of the Ki67 nuclear proliferation antigen in formalin-fixed paraffin-embedded (FFPE) tissue sections, frozen cryosections, and cultured cells.

Biological Significance & Proliferation Marker:

  • Cell Cycle-Specific Nuclear Antigen: Ki67 protein is strictly expressed in all active phases of the cell division cycle ($G_1, S, G_2$, and mitosis $M$), but is completely down-regulated in resting/quiescent cells in the $G_0$ phase.
  • Broad 11-Species Cross-Reactivity: The optimized anti-Ki67 antibody demonstrates robust immunoreactivity in human, mouse, rat, sheep, rabbit, horse, cow, dog, pig, monkey, and Chinese hamster tissues.
  • Polymer-HRP Detection System: Employs a polymeric horseradish peroxidase (HRP)-conjugated secondary antibody that eliminates non-specific background caused by endogenous biotin in liver, kidney, and metabolic tissues, providing crisp nuclear contrast with 3,3'-diaminobenzidine (DAB).

format_list_numbered Usage Procedure

Paraffin Deparaffinization & Heat-Induced Epitope Retrieval (HIER)

1

Deparaffinization in Xylene

Immerse FFPE slide racks in a staining dish containing fresh xylene for 3 changes of 5 minutes each (3 × 5 min).

schedule 15 mins
2

Graded Ethanol Rehydration

Rehydrate tissue sections through graded alcohols: • 100% Ethanol: 2 changes of 2 minutes each (2 × 2 min) • 95% Ethanol: 2 changes of 2 minutes each (2 × 2 min) • Distilled / deionized water (ddH2O): Rinse thoroughly for 2 minutes.

infoNever allow slides to dry out once deparaffinization has begun.
schedule 10 mins
3

1× Antigen Retrieval Working Solution Preparation

Dilute 10 mL of 10× Ag Retrieval Solution (VB-4002D-1) with 90 mL of ddH2O to prepare 100 mL of 1× Ag retrieval working solution. Mix thoroughly.

schedule 2 mins
4

Microwave Heat-Induced Epitope Retrieval (HIER)

Place rehydrated slides into a Coplin jar containing the 1× Ag retrieval solution. Heat in a microwave oven for 2–3 minutes to bring solution to a boil. Maintain at a sub-boiling temperature for 10 minutes (using power adjustments, pressure cooker, or 95–98°C water bath). Remove jar and allow slides to cool slowly on the benchtop for 30 minutes at room temperature.

infoCRITICAL: Do NOT use heat-induced antigen retrieval on frozen sections or cultured cells.
schedule 45 mins

Cryosection & Cultured Cell Line Preparation

1

Cultured Cell Lines on Coverslips / Chamber Slides

Grow cells on sterile glass coverslips or multi-well chamber slides overnight at 37°C. Wash briefly with 1X PBS. Fix according to desired method: • Option A: 10% Neutral Buffered Formalin in PBS for 20 minutes at RT (keep wet) • Option B: Ice-cold Methanol for 10 minutes, allow to air dry for 10 minutes • Option C: Ice-cold Acetone for 10 minutes, allow to air dry for 10 minutes Wash in 1X PBS and proceed directly to Staining Procedure (Skip Heat Retrieval).

schedule 30 mins
2

Cryosections (Frozen Tissue Preparation)

Snap freeze fresh surgical or animal tissue in liquid nitrogen (or isopentane pre-cooled in LN2). Embed in OCT compound in cryomolds and store at -80°C. Equilibrate tissue block to cryostat temperature (-20°C). Cut 5–10 µm cryosections and mount onto charged Superfrost Plus slides.

schedule 30 mins
3

Cryosection Fixation & Hydration

Warm frozen slides at room temperature for 30 minutes. Fix in ice-cold acetone or ice-cold methanol for 10 minutes. Air dry for 30 minutes. Rinse in 1X PBS for 2 × 2 minutes. Proceed directly to Staining Procedure without heat antigen retrieval.

schedule 70 mins

Polymer HRP-Conjugated DAB Immunohistochemical Staining

1

Permeabilization & Hydrophobic Barrier

Rinse sections in PBS-Triton X-100 (0.025%) for 2 changes of 2 minutes (2 × 2 min). Carefully wipe excess buffer from around the tissue perimeter with a Kimwipe and draw a tight hydrophobic circle using a Mini PAP Pen.

schedule 5 mins
2

Serum Blocking (Non-Specific Binding Block)

Apply 3–4 drops of RTU Blocking Buffer (VB-4002D-2) to completely cover the tissue section. Incubate in a humidified chamber for 30 minutes at room temperature to block non-specific immunoglobulin binding sites.

schedule 30 mins
3

Primary Antibody Incubation (RTU Anti-Ki67)

Tap off blocking buffer (do not rinse). Immediately apply 3–4 drops of RTU Anti-Ki67 Primary Antibody (VB-4002D-3). Incubate for 1–2 hours at room temperature, or overnight at 4°C in a humidified chamber. Rinse in 1X PBS for 3 × 2 minutes.

schedule 1–2 hours (or O/N)
4

Endogenous Peroxidase Blocking (Optional / Recommended)

For tissues with high endogenous peroxidase activity (e.g. spleen, liver, kidney, blood-rich specimens), incubate sections in 0.3% H2O2 in PBS (or 0.3% H2O2 in methanol) for 10 minutes at room temperature. Rinse in 1X PBS for 2 × 2 minutes.

schedule 10 mins
5

Polymeric Peroxidase Secondary Antibody Incubation

Apply 3–4 drops of RTU Polymeric Peroxidase (HRP)-Conjugated Secondary Antibody (VB-4002D-4) to each section. Incubate in a humidified chamber for 30 minutes at room temperature. Rinse in 1X PBS for 3 changes of 2 minutes (3 × 2 min).

infoPolymer detection provides high sensitivity and completely avoids endogenous biotin background.
schedule 30 mins
6

Working DAB Solution Preparation

Mix 25 µL of DAB Stock Solution (40×, VB-4002D-5) with 1.0 mL of Stable DAB Buffer (VB-4002D-6). Mix thoroughly by vortexing immediately before use.

infoPrepare DAB working solution fresh. Discard any unused working solution after 6 hours.
schedule 2 mins
7

DAB Chromogen Substrate Incubation

Apply 3 drops of prepared DAB working solution to cover the tissue. Incubate for 2–8 minutes. Closely monitor brown nuclear signal development under a light microscope. As soon as distinct brown nuclear staining appears with minimal background, terminate reaction by immersing slides in ddH2O for 2 × 2 minutes.

infoWARNING: DAB is a potential carcinogen. Wear nitrile gloves and dispose of in toxic chemical waste.
schedule 2–8 mins
8

Hematoxylin Nuclear Counterstaining

Apply 3 drops of RTU Hematoxylin Solution (VB-4002D-7) for 1–2 minutes. Rinse slides gently in running tap water for 2 changes of 2 minutes (2 × 2 min) to allow blueing of non-proliferating nuclei.

schedule 5 mins
9

Dehydration, Clearing & Coverslipping

Dehydrate through graded ethanol series: • 75% Ethanol: 2 minutes • 95% Ethanol: 2 minutes • 100% Ethanol: 2 changes of 3 minutes each (2 × 3 min) Clear in xylene for 2 changes of 5 minutes each (2 × 5 min). Apply 1–2 drops of permanent resinous mounting medium (e.g. DPX or Permount) and coverslip without trapping air bubbles.

schedule 20 mins

Ki67 Proliferation Index (%) Quantification & Scoring Protocols

1

Low-Power Survey & Hotspot Identification

Examine the entire stained section under low magnification (40×–100×) to assess overall staining homogeneity and identify 'hotspots' (areas with the highest density of Ki67-positive brown nuclei).

schedule 5 mins
2

High-Power Counting Methodology (400× HPF)

Under high magnification (400× HPF), count both Ki67-positive brown nuclei and Ki67-negative blue nuclei across 3 to 5 representative hotspot fields. A minimum of 500 to 1,000 total tumor cells should be evaluated to ensure statistical validity.

schedule 15 mins
3

Ki67 Proliferation Index Calculation Formula

Calculate the Ki67 Labeling / Proliferation Index using the standard formula: Ki67 Index (%) = (Number of Ki67-positive tumor nuclei / Total number of tumor nuclei) × 100%

schedule 5 mins
4

Clinical Stratification & Grading Consensus

• Neuroendocrine Neoplasms (WHO 2022 Grading): - Grade 1 (G1): Ki67 Index < 3% - Grade 2 (G2): Ki67 Index 3% – 20% - Grade 3 (G3 / NEC): Ki67 Index > 20% • Breast Carcinoma (St. Gallen Consensus): - Luminal A-like: Ki67 < 14%–20% (Low proliferation) - Luminal B-like (HER2-negative): Ki67 ≥ 20% (High proliferation) • Lymphomas & Solid Tumors: > 25% indicates high proliferative fraction.

schedule Classification

Quality Controls & Troubleshooting Guide (VB-4002D)

1

Quality Control Slides (Positive & Negative Controls)

• Positive Control: Run the included Ki67 Positive Control FFPE Slides (VB-4002D-8, e.g. tonsil, lymph node, or proliferating tumor) in parallel with test specimens to verify reagent reactivity. • Negative Control: In parallel, incubate one test slide with 1X PBS or an isotype-matched non-immune IgG instead of the primary antibody to evaluate non-specific background.

schedule Control QC
2

High Background Staining

• Cause 1: Incomplete endogenous peroxidase blocking. -> Remedy: Incubate in 0.3% H2O2 in methanol or PBS for 10–30 min at RT. • Cause 2: Incomplete deparaffinization. -> Remedy: Prepare new sections and deparaffinize in fresh xylene (3 × 5 min). • Cause 3: Inadequate washing between steps. -> Remedy: Rinse gently with wash buffer bottle and soak in wash bath for 5 min with gentle agitation. • Cause 4: Over-development of DAB substrate. -> Remedy: Reduce DAB incubation time (2–5 min) and monitor under microscope. • Cause 5: Specimen dehydration during staining. -> Remedy: Keep sections moist in humidified chamber at all times.

schedule Remedy
3

Negative Staining on Positive Slides

• Cause 1: Protocol steps performed in incorrect sequence or antibody omitted. -> Remedy: Repeat procedure carefully. • Cause 2: Heat-labile antigens destroyed during tissue processing. -> Remedy: Use freshly cut slides (< 2 weeks old). Ensure paraffin melting temperature does not exceed 55–58°C (embedding wax must NOT exceed 60°C). • Cause 3: Specimen improperly fixed or over-fixed in formalin. -> Remedy: Optimize fixation (10% NBF for 18–24 hours; avoid > 48h over-fixation). • Cause 4: Epitopes masked due to inadequate antigen retrieval. -> Remedy: Verify microwave boiling time and maintain 10 min sub-boiling.

schedule Remedy
4

Weak Staining on All Slides

• Cause 1: Specimen retained excess wash liquid, diluting antibody. -> Remedy: Carefully wipe excess liquid around tissue before applying reagent drops. • Cause 2: Primary or secondary antibody incubation time insufficient. -> Remedy: Prolong primary antibody incubation to 2 hours at RT or overnight at 4°C. • Cause 3: Incomplete deparaffinization creating a hydrophobic wax barrier. -> Remedy: Use fresh xylene and fresh 100% ethanol baths.

schedule Remedy

Storage & Stability

Store entire kit at 2–8°C upon arrival. Do not freeze polymeric conjugate.

event_available Stable for 12 months when stored properly at 2–8°C.

warning Cautions & Safety

  • check_circle DAB is a suspected carcinogen; handle in a chemical fume hood with double nitrile gloves and neutralize liquid waste with 10% sodium hypochlorite bleach before disposal.
  • check_circle Do NOT use heat-induced antigen retrieval on frozen sections or cultured cells; heat damages cell morphology.
  • check_circle Never allow slides to dry out during any step of the staining protocol; keep sections in a humidified slide chamber.
  • check_circle Always include positive control slides (VB-4002D-8) and negative control slides (omitting primary antibody) in every staining run.
analytics

Expected Results

Crisp dark brown nuclear immunoreactivity localized to nucleoli and chromatin in actively proliferating cells with virtually zero background, contrasting sharply against blue hematoxylin-stained non-proliferating nuclei.
