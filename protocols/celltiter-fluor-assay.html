CellTiter-Fluor™ Cell Viability Assay

biotech Promega Corporation • Cat. # G6080, G6081, G6082

info Overview & Principle

The CellTiter-Fluor™ Cell Viability Assay (Promega TB371) is a non-lytic, single-reagent fluorescence assay that measures the relative number of live cells in culture. It uses a cell-permeant fluorogenic peptide substrate (glycyl-phenylalanyl-aminofluorocoumarin; GF-AFC) that readily penetrates intact cell membranes.

Assay Principle: Endogenous intracellular aminopeptidase activity in metabolically viable cells cleaves the non-fluorescent GF-AFC substrate to release free AFC, generating a bright fluorescent signal ($380 ext{--}400 ext{ nm}$ excitation / $505 ext{ nm}$ emission) directly proportional to the number of living cells. Because the assay is completely non-lytic, the exact same cell samples can be multiplexed with downstream luminescent assays (e.g. Caspase-Glo® 3/7, CytoTox-Glo™, CellTiter-Glo®) or nucleic acid extraction.

format_list_numbered Usage Procedure

96-Well Format Standard Viability Protocol

1

Reagent Reconstitution (5X Working Solution)

Thaw GF-AFC Substrate and Assay Buffer in a 37°C water bath. Transfer 10 µL of GF-AFC Substrate into 2.0 mL of Assay Buffer (for 5X reagent), or into 10 mL of Assay Buffer (for 2X reagent). Vortex thoroughly for 10 seconds until completely dissolved.

infoReconstituted CellTiter-Fluor™ reagent is stable for 24 hours at room temperature or 7 days at 4°C.
schedule 5 mins
2

Cell Culture & Plating

Plate adherent or suspension cells in 100 µL of culture medium per well in black-walled, clear-bottom 96-well plates. Include cell-free medium wells as negative blank controls. Allow cells to adhere and treat with test compounds as desired.

schedule Overnight / Variable
3

Reagent Addition (5X Protocol)

Add 20 µL of 5X CellTiter-Fluor™ Reagent directly to each well containing 100 µL of cells in culture medium (1:5 dilution). (Alternatively, add 50 µL of 2X Reagent to 50 µL of cells).

schedule 5 mins
4

Plate Mixing & Incubation

Mix plate on an orbital plate shaker for 30–60 seconds at 300–500 rpm to ensure homogenous reagent distribution. Incubate at 37°C in 5% CO2 for 30 minutes to 3 hours (typically 30–90 minutes).

schedule 30–90 mins
5

Fluorescence Measurement

Measure fluorescence in a microplate reader using 380–400 nm excitation (optimal 390 nm) and 505 nm emission (filter bandwidth 505 nm or 510–570 nm). Subtract average blank fluorescence from sample values.

schedule 5 mins

384-Well High-Throughput Screening Protocol

1

Miniaturized Plating

Plate cells at 500–5,000 cells in 20 µL culture medium per well in black-walled 384-well microplates. Treat with compounds.

schedule Variable
2

5X Reagent Addition

Add 5 µL of 5X CellTiter-Fluor™ Reagent to each well (total volume 25 µL). Mix plate briefly on an orbital shaker.

schedule 2 mins
3

Incubation & High-Throughput Readout

Incubate plate at 37°C for 30–90 minutes. Measure fluorescence at 390 nm excitation / 505 nm emission.

schedule 30–90 mins

Downstream Luminescent Multiplexing Protocols

1

Multiplexing with Caspase-Glo® 3/7 (Apoptosis)

Following CellTiter-Fluor™ fluorescence measurement, immediately add 100 µL of Caspase-Glo® 3/7 Reagent directly to the exact same 96 wells. Incubate at room temperature for 30–60 minutes, then measure luminescence on a luminometer to quantify apoptosis normalized to viable cell number.

schedule 30–60 mins
2

Multiplexing with CytoTox-Glo™ (Cytotoxicity)

Add 50 µL of CytoTox-Glo™ AAF-Glo substrate directly following fluorescence readout to determine dead cell protease activity, achieving simultaneous viability + cytotoxicity profiling in a single well.

schedule 15 mins
3

Multiplexing with CellTiter-Glo® (ATP Quantification)

Add 100 µL of CellTiter-Glo® Luminescent Reagent to the same well to lyse cells and quantify cellular ATP pools.

schedule 10 mins

calculate Fluorescence Background Subtraction & Viability Normalization

Net Fluorescence (AFU) = F_sample - F_blank Relative Viability (%) = (F_treated - F_blank) / (F_vehicle - F_blank) × 100%

1. Blank Correction: $F_{blank}$ is the average fluorescence signal from cell-free medium controls containing the exact same concentration of CellTiter-Fluor™ reagent.

2. Optical Parameters: Excitation: $380 ext{--}400 ext{ nm}$ ($390 ext{ nm}$ optimal); Emission: $505 ext{ nm}$ (cutoff $510 ext{--}570 ext{ nm}$).

3. Multiplex Normalization: $ ext{Normalized Apoptosis} = ext{Caspase Luminescence (RLU)} / ext{CellTiter-Fluor (AFU)}$.

Storage & Stability

Store at -20°C upon receipt. Protect GF-AFC substrate and Assay Buffer from light and moisture.

event_available Stable until expiration date printed on label when stored at -20°C.

warning Cautions & Safety

  • check_circle Use black-walled, clear-bottom plates to prevent well-to-well optical cross-talk and autofluorescence.
  • check_circle Do not use serum concentrations exceeding 10% without verifying background, as serum aminopeptidases may cause slight baseline signal.
  • check_circle Avoid high concentrations of primary amine-containing or highly colored test compounds that absorb at 380–505 nm.
