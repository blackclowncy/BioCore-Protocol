schedule Section 3

Emergency First Aid Measures & Spill Response

1

Emergency First Aid Protocols (Eyes, Skin, Inhalation, Ingestion)

• Eye Contact: Immediately flush eyes with plenty of running water for at least 15–20 minutes, lifting upper and lower eyelids. Seek immediate medical attention / ophthalmologist evaluation. • Skin Contact: Immediately flush contaminated skin with soap and copious water for at least 15 minutes while removing contaminated clothing and shoes. Wash clothing before reuse. • Inhalation: Remove exposed person to fresh air immediately. If breathing is difficult, give oxygen. If not breathing, perform artificial respiration (CPR). Seek prompt medical care. • Ingestion: Never give anything by mouth to an unconscious person. Rinse mouth thoroughly with water. Do NOT induce vomiting unless specifically directed by medical personnel. Call Poison Center immediately.

schedule Section 4
2

Firefighting Measures & Extinguishing Media

• Suitable Extinguishing Media: Water spray, alcohol-resistant foam, dry chemical powder, or carbon dioxide (CO2). • Specific Hazards: Thermal decomposition may release toxic or irritating fumes (Carbon oxides, Nitrogen oxides, Halogenated gases, Sulfur oxides). • Firefighter Protection: Wear self-contained breathing apparatus (SCBA) with full protective turnout gear.

schedule Section 5
3

Accidental Release & Spill Clean-Up SOP

• Personal Precautions: Evacuate non-essential personnel. Eliminate all ignition sources. Ensure adequate ventilation. Don full PPE (gloves, goggles, lab coat, respirator if vapors present). • Environmental Precautions: Prevent product from entering drains, sewers, surface water, or ground water. • Spill Containment & Clean-Up: Soak up liquid spill with inert absorbent material (vermiculite, dry sand, or commercial spill pillows). For solids, sweep up gently avoiding dust generation. Place in suitable, labeled, closed hazardous waste disposal containers.

schedule Section 6

Safe Handling Guidelines, Storage Conditions & Exposure Controls

1

Safe Handling & Operational Practices

• Avoid contact with eyes, skin, and clothing. Avoid breathing vapors, mists, or dusts. • Handle inside a certified chemical fume hood or well-ventilated enclosure. • Wash hands thoroughly with soap and water after handling and before eating, drinking, or smoking. • Keep away from open flames, hot surfaces, and incompatible chemicals.

schedule Section 7
2

Storage Conditions & Incompatibilities

• Storage Temperature: Store refrigerated at 2–8°C. Do not freeze. • Packaging: Keep container tightly closed in a dry, cool, and well-ventilated location. • Segregation: Store away from strong oxidizing agents, strong acids, strong bases, and direct heat sources. • Security: Keep out of reach of unauthorized personnel in dedicated laboratory chemical storage cabinets.

schedule Section 7
3

Engineering Controls & Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)

• Engineering Controls: Safety eyewash station and emergency deluge shower must be accessible within 10 seconds of work area. Chemical fume hood with face velocity >= 100 fpm. • Eye/Face Protection: Tight-fitting chemical splash goggles (ANSI Z87.1 approved) and face shield when splash hazard exists. • Skin/Hand Protection: Impermeable nitrile or neoprene gloves; inspect gloves before use; remove using glove-to-glove technique. • Body Protection: Flame-retardant / chemical-resistant laboratory coat. • Respiratory Protection: NIOSH-approved respirator (N95 particulate / organic vapor combination) if exposure limits are exceeded or irritation occurs.
