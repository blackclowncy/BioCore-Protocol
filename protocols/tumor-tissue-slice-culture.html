Organotypic Tumor Slice Cultures (TSCs) Protocol

biotech Kenerson et al. / Univ. of Washington • DOI: 10.1016/j.xpro.2021.100590

info Overview & Principle

The Organotypic Tumor Slice Cultures (TSCs) Protocol (STAR Protocols 2, 100574) provides a comprehensive method for preparing and culturing live, 250–300 µm thick precision-cut slices from human and murine solid tumors (e.g. liver, colorectal, breast, lung, pancreatic cancer).

Biological Significance: Unlike 2D monolayer cultures or single-cell organoids, organotypic slice cultures preserve intact 3D tumor architecture, functional stromal-immune microenvironment (T cells, macrophages, fibroblasts), extracellular matrix stiffness, and vascular niches for ex vivo drug screening, immunotherapy testing, and multi-omics profiling.

format_list_numbered Usage Procedure

Fresh Specimen Procurement & Low-Melting Agarose Embedding

1

Fresh Tissue Procurement & Cold Ischemia Minimization

Collect fresh surgical human tumor specimens immediately after resection into sterile 5 mL tubes containing 3 mL ice-cold Belzer UW solution or collection buffer. Transport immediately to the laboratory on melting ice (cold ischemia time < 1 hour).

schedule 15–30 mins
2

Tumor Core Trimming

In a sterile laminar flow hood, transfer specimen to a cold sterile Petri dish. Trim necrotic or fat margins with a sterile scalpel into uniform cylindrical cores of 5–8 mm diameter and 10 mm height.

schedule 10 mins
3

Low-Melting Agarose Embedding

Prepare 2% (w/v) low-melting agarose in sterile 1X PBS. Autoclave and place in a 37–40°C water bath. Position tumor core vertically in a square plastic casting mold. Pour 37–40°C molten agarose over specimen to completely cover it. Transfer mold to melting ice for 5 minutes to solidify.

infoAgarose temperature must NOT exceed 42°C to prevent thermal tumor cell death.
schedule 15 mins

Vibrating Blade Microtome Slicing Protocol

1

Vibratome Setup & Blade Calibration

Mount sterile double-edge stainless steel razor blade (or sapphire blade) on the Leica VT1200S vibratome holder. Fill the buffer tray with ice-cold cutting buffer and surround with ice. Set blade clearance angle to 15°–18°.

schedule 10 mins
2

Specimen Mounting & Parameter Configuration

Glue the trimmed agarose-embedded tumor block onto the specimen disc using cyanoacrylate adhesive (Loctite 401). Configure slicing parameters: • Slice Thickness: 250–300 µm • Advance Speed: 0.05–0.10 mm/s • Vibration Amplitude: 1.0–1.2 mm • Vibration Frequency: 85 Hz

schedule 5 mins
3

Slice Collection

Initiate automated slicing. Use a sterile curved spatula or wide-bore glass Pasteur pipette to gently lift each floating slice into a 6-well plate containing ice-cold wash media.

schedule 15–30 mins

Organotypic Air-Liquid Interface Culture (5–7 Days)

1

Plate & Insert Preparation

Place Millicell organotypic membrane inserts (0.4 µm pore size) into 6-well culture plates. Add 1.0–1.2 mL of complete TSC medium to the lower chamber underneath each insert.

infoEnsure fluid touches only the underside of the insert without submerging the upper surface.
schedule 5 mins
2

Slice Placement & Air-Liquid Interface

Transfer 2–3 tumor slices flat onto the top membrane of each insert. Gently remove excess liquid around slice perimeter with a P200 pipette tip to establish an air-liquid interface (direct oxygen diffusion from air; nutrient supply from bottom).

schedule 5 mins
3

Incubation & Media Exchange

Incubate in a 37°C, 5% CO2 humidified incubator (atmospheric or hyperoxic 80% O2). Replace lower chamber medium every 24–48 hours.

schedule 5–7 Days

Drug Screening & Multi-Omics Endpoints

1

Viability Staining (LIVE/DEAD & CellTiter-Glo 3D)

Incubate slices with 2 µM Calcein AM + 4 µM EthD-1 for 45 min at RT, then image with confocal microscopy to quantify live/dead ratio across slice depth (Z-stack). Alternatively, homogenize in CellTiter-Glo® 3D for ATP readout.

schedule 60 mins
2

Histology & Immunofluorescence (IHC / IF)

Fix slices in 4% PFA for 2 hours, embed in paraffin, and stain with H&E, Ki-67 (proliferation), cleaved caspase-3 (apoptosis), and CD3/CD8/CD68 (immune infiltrating subsets).

schedule Variable
3

Supernatant ELISA & Single-Cell RNA-Seq

Collect culture supernatants for cytokine/chemokine profiling (IFN-γ, TNF-α, IL-6), or dissociate slices enzymatically for scRNA-seq tumor heterogeneity analysis.

schedule Variable

Storage & Stability

Store slice culture media at 4°C. Store agarose and additives according to individual specifications.

event_available Slice culture functional viability maintained for 5–7 days in culture.

warning Cautions & Safety

  • check_circle Maintain strict sterile technique throughout procurement, sectioning, and culture.
  • check_circle Do not allow agarose temperature to exceed 42°C during tissue embedding.
  • check_circle Ensure medium volume touches only the underside of the Millicell membrane; submerged slices experience rapid hypoxia.
analytics

Expected Results

Preserves > 80% viable tumor, stromal, and immune cells for 5–7 days ex vivo, demonstrating robust differential sensitivity to targeted therapies and immune checkpoint blockade.
