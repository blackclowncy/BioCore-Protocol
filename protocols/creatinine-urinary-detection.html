Creatinine Urinary Detection Kit

biotech Invitrogen / Thermo Fisher Scientific • Catalog Number EIACUN (192 tests)

info Overview & Principle

The Creatinine Urinary Detection Kit is designed to detect and quantify the level of creatinine in urine samples across multiple species including human, rat, dog, and monkey. Urinary creatinine excretion is relatively constant under normal conditions and serves as the gold standard for normalizing excretion rates of other urine biomarkers, drugs, and electrolytes.

Assay Principle: Creatinine reacts with alkaline picrate (Jaffé reaction) to produce an orange-red tautomer that absorbs strongly at 490 nm. The reaction rate and final intensity are directly proportional to creatinine concentration.

Wavelength

format_list_numbered Usage Procedure

Urinary Sample Assay Procedure

1

Sample Clarification & Dilution

Centrifuge urine samples at 2,000 × g for 5 minutes to pellet cellular debris. Dilute urine 1:10 to 1:20 in 1X Assay Buffer (or deionized water).

infoMouse urine is not recommended as active tubular secretion accounts for >50% of excretion.
schedule 10 mins
2

Standard Curve Dilutions

Perform serial 1:2 dilutions of the 100 mg/dL standard down to 0.156 mg/dL in 1X Assay Buffer (Standards: 10, 5, 2.5, 1.25, 0.625, 0.312, 0.156 mg/dL).

schedule 10 mins
3

Plate Loading

Pipette 50 µL of standards, blanks (Assay Buffer), and diluted urine samples in duplicate into clear 96-well plate wells.

schedule 5 mins
4

Add Creatinine Reagent

Add 100 µL of Creatinine Reagent to each well using a multichannel pipette. Tap plate gently to mix.

schedule 2 mins
5

Incubation & Measurement

Incubate plate at room temperature for 30 minutes. Read optical absorbance at 490 nm on a microplate reader.

schedule 30 mins

calculate Creatinine Concentration Calculation

Urinary Creatinine (mg/dL) = [(OD_sample - OD_blank) - b] / a × Dilution_Factor

Calculate 4-parameter logistic (4PL) or linear regression from standard curve. Multiply by dilution factor (e.g. ×10 or ×20) to obtain actual concentration in urine sample.

inventory_2

Storage & Stability

Store kit at -20°C upon arrival. Once opened, store components at 4°C and use within 2 weeks.

event_available Stable until expiration date when stored at -20°C.

warning Cautions & Safety

  • check_circle Store Creatinine Reagent at 4°C protected from light; allow to equilibrate to RT before use.
  • check_circle Mouse urine samples are incompatible due to unique murine renal physiology (tubular secretion).
  • check_circle Alkaline picrate will stain skin and surfaces yellow; handle with nitrile gloves.
analytics

Expected Results

Normal human urinary creatinine typically ranges from 20 to 300 mg/dL depending on hydration status and muscle mass.
