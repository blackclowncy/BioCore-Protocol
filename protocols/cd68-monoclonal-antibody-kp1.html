CD68 Monoclonal Antibody (Clone KP1)

biotech Invitrogen / eBioscience™ • Catalog 14-0688-82 / MA5-13324 (100 µg / 200 µL)

info Overview & Principle

CD68 Monoclonal Antibody (Clone KP1, eBioscience™ / Invitrogen Cat. No. 14-0688-82 / MA5-13324) is a benchmark monoclonal antibody directed against the human CD68 antigen (Macrosialin / gp110 / Scavenger Receptor D), widely regarded as the gold-standard lineage marker for tissue macrophages, monocytes, osteoclasts, and myeloid-derived phagocytes.

Biological Target & Epitope Characteristics:

  • Intracellular Lysosomal Localization: CD68 is a 110 kDa heavily glycosylated type I integral membrane protein belonging to the lysosomal-associated membrane protein (LAMP) family. It resides predominantly within the late endosomal and lysosomal membranes of monocytes and macrophages, participating in intracellular lysosomal metabolism, antigen processing, and phagocytic clearance.
  • Robust Formalin-Resistant Epitope (Clone KP1): Generated by immunizing mice with a subcellular fraction of human alveolar macrophages. Clone KP1 recognizes a robust, formalin-resistant peptide epitope on CD68, ensuring exceptional fidelity in formalin-fixed paraffin-embedded (FFPE) pathology specimens.
  • Broad Cross-Species Reactivity: Highly reactive with Human, Cat, Non-human primate, Rat, Cynomolgus monkey, Rabbit, Pig, Hamster, and Mouse tissues (>100 peer-reviewed publications). (Note: Non-reactive with Canine or Chicken).
  • Multi-Platform Validation: Optimized for IHC-P (1–5 µg/mL), IHC-F (1 µg/mL), ICC/IF confocal microscopy (5 µg/mL), intracellular flow cytometry (0.5–1 µg/test), and Western blotting (1–2 µg/mL).

format_list_numbered Usage Procedure

Immunohistochemistry on Formalin-Fixed Paraffin-Embedded Sections (IHC-P)

1

Deparaffinization & Rehydration

Immerse slide racks in fresh xylene for 3 changes of 5 minutes each (3 × 5 min). Rehydrate through graded ethanol baths: 100% ethanol (2 × 2 min), 95% ethanol (2 × 2 min), 70% ethanol (2 min), and rinse in distilled water for 2 minutes.

schedule 20 mins
2

Heat-Induced Epitope Retrieval (HIER)

Immerse slides in 10 mM Sodium Citrate buffer (pH 6.0) or 1 mM EDTA buffer (pH 8.0/9.0). Heat in a microwave oven or pressure cooker to a boil (2–3 min), then maintain at sub-boiling temperature (95–98°C) for 10–15 minutes. Cool slides slowly on the benchtop for 30 minutes at room temperature. Rinse in 1X PBS for 2 × 2 minutes.

infoHIER in pH 6.0 citrate provides optimal unmasking of the KP1 lysosomal epitope.
schedule 45 mins
3

Endogenous Peroxidase & Non-Specific Blocking

Incubate sections in 0.3% H2O2 in PBS (or methanol) for 10–15 minutes at room temperature to quench endogenous peroxidases. Rinse in 1X PBS for 2 × 2 minutes. Apply 5% normal goat serum (or 1% BSA + 0.1% Triton X-100 in PBS) for 30–60 minutes at room temperature in a humidified chamber.

schedule 45 mins
4

Primary Antibody Incubation (KP1)

Dilute CD68 KP1 antibody stock (0.5 mg/mL) to a working concentration of 1.0–5.0 µg/mL (1:100 to 1:500 dilution) in 1X PBS containing 1% BSA. Apply 100–150 µL per section. Incubate for 1–2 hours at room temperature, or overnight at 4°C in a humidified chamber. Rinse in 1X PBS for 3 changes of 2 minutes (3 × 2 min).

schedule 1–2 hours (or O/N)
5

Polymer Secondary Detection & DAB Chromogen

Apply polymer HRP-conjugated anti-mouse secondary antibody for 30 minutes at room temperature. Rinse in 1X PBS for 3 × 2 minutes. Apply fresh DAB working substrate and monitor brown lysosomal/cytoplasmic staining under a microscope (typically 2–8 minutes). Terminate reaction in ddH2O.

schedule 40 mins
6

Counterstain & Mounting

Counterstain in Mayer's Hematoxylin for 1–2 minutes, blue in running tap water for 2 minutes. Dehydrate through 95% and 100% ethanol, clear in xylene (2 × 5 min), and coverslip with permanent resinous mounting medium.

schedule 15 mins

Immunohistochemistry on Frozen Cryosections (IHC-F)

1

Cryosection Preparation & Fixation

Equilibrate frozen tissue blocks (-80°C) to cryostat chamber (-20°C). Cut 5–8 µm cryosections and mount onto Superfrost Plus slides. Air dry at room temperature for 30 minutes. Fix slides in ice-cold acetone or acetone/methanol (1:1) for 10 minutes at 4°C. Air dry for 20 minutes.

schedule 60 mins
2

Washing & Permeabilization / Blocking

Rinse fixed slides in 1X PBS for 2 × 2 minutes. Block non-specific binding by applying 5% normal goat serum + 0.1% Triton X-100 in PBS for 30 minutes at room temperature.

infoTriton X-100 permeabilizes lysosomal membranes to maximize KP1 antibody access.
schedule 30 mins
3

Primary Antibody Incubation & Visualization

Dilute CD68 KP1 antibody to 1.0 µg/mL (1:500 dilution) in 1% BSA/PBS. Incubate for 1 hour at room temperature or overnight at 4°C. Rinse in PBS (3 × 2 min). Apply HRP polymer secondary antibody or fluorophore-conjugated anti-mouse IgG (e.g. Alexa Fluor 488/594 at 2 µg/mL). Develop DAB or mount with antifade coverslip.

schedule 1–2 hours

Immunocytochemistry & Immunofluorescence on Cultured Macrophages (ICC/IF)

1

Cell Culture & Macrophage Differentiation

Seed monocyte cell lines (e.g. THP-1 or U937) onto sterile glass coverslips in 24-well plates. Differentiate into M0 macrophages using 50–100 nM Phorbol 12-myristate 13-acetate (PMA) for 24–48 hours, followed by 24h rest in fresh medium.

schedule 48–72 hours
2

Fixation & Permeabilization

Wash cells gently with 1X PBS. Fix in 4% Paraformaldehyde (PFA) in PBS for 10–15 minutes at room temperature. Wash with PBS (2 × 2 min). Permeabilize with 0.1% Triton X-100 in PBS for 15 minutes at room temperature.

schedule 30 mins
3

Blocking & Primary Antibody Incubation

Block non-specific sites with 1% BSA + 0.1% Tween-20 in PBS for 1 hour at room temperature. Incubate with CD68 KP1 antibody diluted to 5.0 µg/mL (1:100) in blocking buffer overnight at 4°C (or 2 hours at room temperature).

schedule 2 hours to O/N
4

Fluorescent Secondary & Counterstaining

Wash 3 × 5 min in PBS-T. Incubate with Goat anti-Mouse IgG Superclonal™ Alexa Fluor 488 (2 µg/mL) + Rhodamine Phalloidin (for F-actin) for 1 hour at room temperature in the dark. Wash 3 × 5 min. Counterstain nuclei with DAPI (1 µg/mL) for 5 minutes. Mount coverslips onto slides using ProLong™ Glass Antifade Mountant.

schedule 90 mins

Intracellular Flow Cytometry Staining SOP

1

Harvest & Surface Staining

Harvest 1 × 10⁶ single-cell suspension of PBMCs, splenocytes, or cultured macrophages. Wash with Flow Cytometry Staining Buffer (PBS + 1% FBS + 0.09% NaN3). Perform live/dead fixable viability dye staining (15 min at 4°C). Perform surface marker staining (e.g. CD14, CD11b, F4/80) if required.

schedule 30 mins
2

Intracellular Fixation & Permeabilization

Resuspend cell pellet in 100 µL of IC Fixation Buffer (or 4% PFA) and incubate for 20 minutes at room temperature (or 4°C in the dark). Centrifuge at 400 × g for 5 minutes. Resuspend and wash pellet in 1X Permeabilization Buffer (containing saponin or Triton X-100).

infoCD68 is predominantly intracellular; permeabilization is strictly mandatory for detection.
schedule 30 mins
3

Intracellular CD68 KP1 Antibody Staining

Resuspend cells in 100 µL of 1X Permeabilization Buffer containing 0.5–1.0 µg of CD68 KP1 antibody (or conjugated KP1-PE/FITC/APC). Incubate for 30–45 minutes at 4°C in the dark. Wash twice with 1X Permeabilization Buffer (400 × g, 5 min).

schedule 45 mins
4

Secondary Labeling & Cytometer Acquisition

If using purified unconjugated KP1, incubate with fluorochrome-conjugated anti-mouse IgG (e.g. Alexa Fluor 488/647) for 30 minutes at 4°C. Wash twice, resuspend in 300 µL Flow Staining Buffer, and acquire on flow cytometer (gating on singlet live cells -> CD14⁺ / CD68⁺).

schedule 45 mins

Quality Controls & Performance Optimization (Clone KP1)

1

Positive & Negative Quality Controls

• Positive Tissue Controls: Human tonsil (interdigitating reticulum cells / macrophages), spleen red pulp macrophages, liver Kupffer cells, or lung alveolar macrophages. • Negative Isotype Control: Purified Mouse IgG1 kappa isotype control at an identical concentration (1–5 µg/mL) to verify absence of non-specific Fc receptor binding.

schedule QC Standard
2

Troubleshooting: Weak or Absent Staining

• Cause 1: Inadequate heat-induced antigen retrieval in FFPE tissue. -> Remedy: Ensure retrieval buffer reaches 95–98°C for full 10–15 min in pH 6.0 citrate or pH 9.0 Tris-EDTA. • Cause 2: Failure to permeabilize intracellular lysosomal membranes in ICC/Flow. -> Remedy: Include 0.1% Triton X-100 or saponin in all blocking and antibody incubation steps. • Cause 3: Over-fixation in formalin (>48h). -> Remedy: Prolong antigen retrieval time or test enzymatic digestion (proteinase K / pronase).

schedule Remedy
3

Troubleshooting: High Non-Specific Background

• Cause 1: Incomplete endogenous peroxidase blocking in liver/spleen. -> Remedy: Treat sections with 3% H2O2 in methanol for 20 minutes before serum blocking. • Cause 2: Non-specific Fc receptor binding on macrophages. -> Remedy: Use 5–10% normal serum from the host species of the secondary antibody, or add Mouse/Human Fc Block (anti-CD16/CD32).

schedule Remedy

Storage & Stability

Store at 4°C in PBS pH 7.2 with 0.09% sodium azide. Do not freeze.

event_available Stable for 12 months from date of shipment at 4°C.

warning Cautions & Safety

  • check_circle Store antibody stock vial strictly at 4°C. Do NOT freeze; repeated freeze-thaw cycles degrade antibody affinity and cause aggregation.
  • check_circle Contains 0.09% sodium azide preservative. Sodium azide is toxic and inhibits HRP enzyme activity; wash thoroughly before adding HRP conjugates.
  • check_circle CD68 is an intracellular lysosomal antigen; permeabilization with Triton X-100 or saponin is strictly mandatory for immunofluorescence and flow cytometry.
  • check_circle Always utilize heat-induced epitope retrieval (HIER) in citrate pH 6.0 for FFPE tissues to maximize epitope accessibility.
analytics

Expected Results

Strong granular cytoplasmic and lysosomal brown/fluorescent immunoreactivity in tissue macrophages, Kupffer cells, and monocytes, with clear separation from CD68-negative parenchymal cells.
