RealTime-Glo™ MT Cell Viability Assay

biotech Promega Corporation • Cat. # G9711, G9712, G9713

info Overview & Principle

The RealTime-Glo™ MT Cell Viability Assay (Promega TM431) is a non-lytic, bioluminescent assay that measures viable cell number in real time. It monitors the reducing potential of metabolically active cells continuously for up to 72 hours from the same sample well.

Assay Principle: The assay couples an engineered NanoLuc® luciferase enzyme with a cell-permeant pro-substrate (MT Cell Viability Substrate). Live cells with active metabolism reduce the pro-substrate into a NanoLuc substrate. Once reduced, the substrate rapidly diffuses out of the cells into the surrounding culture medium, where it is immediately utilized by extracellular NanoLuc® luciferase to produce a stable, glow-type bioluminescent signal proportional to viable cell number.

Signal Type
Bioluminescence (NanoLuc)
Monitoring Window
Real-time up to 72 hours
Assay Mode
Continuous-read or Endpoint
Mechanism
Cellular Reduction Potential

format_list_numbered Usage Procedure

Continuous Real-Time Monitoring Protocol (Up to 72h)

1

Prepare 2X RealTime-Glo™ Reagent in Medium

Equilibrate MT Cell Viability Substrate and NanoLuc® Enzyme to room temperature. For every 1.0 mL of prewarmed culture medium, add 2.0 µL of MT Substrate (1000X) and 2.0 µL of NanoLuc® Enzyme (1000X) (1:500 dilution to make 2X reagent). Mix thoroughly by vortexing.

infoPrepare 2X reagent immediately before cell plating or compound addition.
schedule 5 mins
2

Cell Seeding & Reagent Addition

Seed cells in 50 µL of culture medium per well in white 96-well microplates (or 10 µL in white 384-well plates). Add 50 µL of 2X RealTime-Glo™ Reagent (or 10 µL in 384-well) directly to each well. Final concentration of both components is 1X.

schedule 10 mins
3

Treatment & Incubation

Add test compounds or vehicle controls. Place microplate in a 37°C, 5% CO2 humidified cell culture incubator.

schedule Variable
4

Continuous / Repeated Luminescent Measurement

Measure luminescence on a standard microplate luminometer (0.25–1.0 second integration time per well) at desired time intervals (e.g. 1h, 2h, 4h, 8h, 12h, 24h, 48h, 72h). Return the plate to the incubator between reads.

schedule 1 min per read

Endpoint Viability Determination Protocol

1

Culture & Treatment

Grow cells in 50–100 µL culture medium in white 96-well plates. Treat with test compounds for the desired incubation period (e.g. 24h, 48h).

schedule Variable
2

2X Reagent Addition at Endpoint

Add an equal volume of 2X RealTime-Glo™ Reagent (50 µL to 50 µL cell culture). Mix on an orbital shaker for 30 seconds.

schedule 2 mins
3

Equilibration & Readout

Incubate plate at 37°C for 10–60 minutes (signal reaches plateau within 10–20 min). Read luminescence on a plate reader.

schedule 10–20 mins

Downstream Multiplexing Capabilities

1

Downstream Apoptosis / Lysis Assays

Because RealTime-Glo™ does not lyse cells or deplete cellular ATP, add Caspase-Glo® 3/7 or CellTiter-Glo® directly to the same wells at the end of the time course.

schedule 30 mins
2

Direct RNA / DNA / Protein Extraction

Aspirate medium or add RNA lysis buffer directly to wells after real-time viability tracking for RT-qPCR or Western blot validation.

schedule Variable

calculate Luminescent Signal Calculation & Blank Correction

Net Luminescence (RLU) = RLU_sample - RLU_blank Viability (% of Control) = (RLU_treated - RLU_blank) / (RLU_vehicle - RLU_blank) × 100%

1. Blank Subtraction: $RLU_{blank}$ is the background luminescence from cell-free medium containing 1X RealTime-Glo™ reagents.

2. Kinetics: Signal increases proportionally with cell division and decreases upon toxic drug insult, providing real-time $IC_{50}$ shifts over time.

Storage & Stability

Store kit components at -20°C upon receipt. Protect NanoLuc Enzyme and MT Cell Viability Substrate from light.

event_available Reagents stable through multiple freeze-thaw cycles without activity loss.

warning Cautions & Safety

  • check_circle Always use white-walled opaque microplates (Costar 3917 or equivalent) to maximize light collection and eliminate optical cross-talk.
  • check_circle NanoLuc® enzyme and substrate must be stored at -20°C and protected from light; minimize freeze-thaw cycles (< 5 cycles).
  • check_circle Do not use luminometer filter cubes; measure unfiltered total bioluminescence.
analytics

Expected Results

Provides a continuous linear dynamic range from 10 to 50,000 cells per well for up to 72 hours with signal-to-background ratio > 50:1.
