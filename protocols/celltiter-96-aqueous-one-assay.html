CellTiter 96® AQueous One Solution Cell Proliferation Assay

biotech Promega Corporation • Cat. # G3580, G3581, G3582

info Overview & Principle

The CellTiter 96® AQueous One Solution Cell Proliferation Assay (Promega TB245) is a colorimetric method for determining the number of viable cells in proliferation, cytotoxicity, or growth factor bioassays. It utilizes a convenient, ready-to-use "One Solution" reagent combining the novel tetrazolium compound MTS and an electron coupling reagent, PES (phenazine ethosulfate).

Assay & Chemical Principle: The MTS tetrazolium compound (Owen's reagent; [3-(4,5-dimethylthiazol-2-yl)-5-(3-carboxymethoxyphenyl)-2-(4-sulfophenyl)-2H-tetrazolium, inner salt]) is bioreduced by metabolically active cells into a colored formazan product. This conversion is accomplished by dehydrogenase enzymes using NADPH or NADH in active mitochondria. The inclusion of PES, which possesses enhanced chemical stability over PMS, allows it to be co-formulated with MTS into a single, light-stable solution without requiring pre-mixing.

Aqueous Solubility Advantage: Unlike traditional MTT assays where the formazan product forms insoluble crystals requiring volatile organic solvents (such as DMSO or isopropanol-HCl) to dissolve, MTS formazan is completely soluble in tissue culture medium. The assay requires no wash steps, no medium aspiration, and no crystal solubilization reagents—delivering a streamlined "add-incubate-measure" homogeneous workflow that can also directly substitute for radioactive [3H]thymidine incorporation.

format_list_numbered Usage Procedure

Standard 96-Well Cell Proliferation & Viability Protocol (Section 3.A)

1

Reagent Thawing & Preparation

Thaw the CellTiter 96® AQueous One Solution Reagent completely before use. A 20 mL bottle requires approximately 90 minutes at room temperature (20–25°C) or 10 minutes in a 37°C water bath. Invert bottle gently to ensure complete homogeneity; protect from prolonged ambient light exposure.

infoReagent performance remains 100% equivalent after up to 10 freeze-thaw cycles.
schedule 10–90 mins
2

Cell Culture & Plating Setup

Plate cells in 100 µL of culture medium per well in standard 96-well clear flat-bottom tissue culture plates (recommended density: 1,000–50,000 cells/well depending on growth rate; 5,000 cells/well for most cell lines). Always include triplicate 'no-cell' blank wells containing 100 µL medium alone to measure background absorbance. Treat cells with test compounds as desired.

schedule 24–72 hours
3

One Solution Reagent Addition

Pipet 20 µL of CellTiter 96® AQueous One Solution Reagent directly into each well containing 100 µL of culture medium using a multichannel or repeating pipette (maintaining the 1:5 volume ratio; final MTS concentration 317 µg/mL). Gently tap or agitate the plate for 10 seconds to ensure uniform mixing.

schedule 5 mins
4

Incubation for Formazan Bioreduction

Incubate the plate at 37°C for 1–4 hours in a humidified, 5% CO2 atmosphere. Metabolically viable cells reduce MTS to soluble formazan, changing the color from yellow/amber to dark brownish-purple. Check absorbance periodically if establishing optimal incubation time for a novel cell line.

schedule 1–4 hours
5

Optional Reaction Stopping (10% SDS)

To read absorbance immediately, proceed directly to Step 6. Alternatively, to halt enzyme reduction and read at a later time, add 25 µL of 10% SDS solution to each well. SDS-treated plates can be stored protected from light in a humidified chamber at room temperature for up to 18 hours before reading.

schedule Optional (up to 18 hrs holding)
6

Absorbance Measurement at 490 nm

Record absorbance at 490 nm using a 96-well plate reader. If a 490 nm filter is unavailable, filters within 450–540 nm can be utilized with minor sensitivity reduction. (Optional: Measure reference wavelength at 630–700 nm to subtract non-specific background from cellular debris, fingerprints, or condensation).

schedule 5 mins

Bioassay of IL-6 Using B9 Hybridoma Cells (Section 3.B)

1

B9 Hybridoma Stock Culture Maintenance

Maintain stock cultures of IL-6-dependent B9 hybridoma cells in RPMI 1640 medium containing 5% FBS, 50 µM 2-mercaptoethanol (2-ME), and 5 ng/mL human recombinant IL-6. Subculture to 2 × 10⁴ cells/mL and refeed every 3 days. Use cells exactly 2 days after the last IL-6 subculture for the assay.

schedule Stock culture
2

Standard Curve & Sample Serial Dilutions

Dispense 50 µL/well of IL-6 samples or standards diluted in RPMI 1640 + 5% FBS + 50 µM 2-ME. Titrate IL-6 standard twofold starting at 4 ng/mL in column 12 down to 4 pg/mL in column 2. Column 1 serves as negative control (medium without IL-6). Equilibrate plate at 37°C, 5% CO2 while harvesting cells.

schedule 20 mins
3

Cell Harvesting, Washing & Resuspension

Harvest B9 cells and wash twice in RPMI 1640 + 5% FBS + 50 µM 2-ME by centrifugation at 300 × g for 5 minutes to remove residual IL-6. Determine cell number and viability (> 95%) by trypan blue exclusion. Resuspend cells to a final working density of 1 × 10⁵ cells/mL.

schedule 25 mins
4

Cell Dispensing & Assay Incubation

Dispense 50 µL of the B9 cell suspension (5,000 cells) into all wells (total well volume = 100 µL; final titrated IL-6 concentrations range from 2 ng/mL in col 12 to 2 pg/mL in col 2). Incubate plate at 37°C for 48–72 hours in a humidified, 5% CO2 incubator.

schedule 48–72 hours
5

One Solution Addition & Color Development

Add 20 µL of CellTiter 96® AQueous One Solution Reagent to each well. Incubate at 37°C in 5% CO2 for 1–4 hours.

schedule 1–4 hours
6

Absorbance Reading & ED50 Calculation

Record absorbance at 490 nm. Subtract no-cell blank absorbance. Plot corrected A490 versus IL-6 concentration. Determine the ED50 value corresponding to one-half the difference between the maximal proliferation plateau and negative control.

schedule 15 mins

Technical Considerations & Assay Optimization (Section 4)

1

Background Absorbance & Reducing Agent Correction

Spontaneous reduction of MTS occurs slowly in culture medium (yielding 0.2–0.3 OD after 4 hours). Strong chemical reducing agents (ascorbic acid, DTT, mercaptoethanol, glutathione, Coenzyme A) can non-enzymatically reduce tetrazolium salts. Always include parallel vehicle-only and compound-only blanks without cells to accurately subtract background.

schedule Quality Control
2

Wavelength Flexibility & Dual-Wavelength Reference

The soluble formazan product has an absorbance peak at 490 nm. Acceptable readings can also be taken at 450–540 nm if a 490 nm filter is unavailable. Dual-wavelength reading with a reference filter at 630–700 nm eliminates non-specific background from plate scratches, optical debris, or fingerprints.

schedule Optical Optimization
3

Cell Density Optimization (Tumor Cells vs. Lymphocytes)

For tumor cell lines, hybridomas, and fibroblasts, seed 5,000 cells/well (detection range spans < 1,000 to > 100,000 cells/well). Primary lymphocytes exhibit lower metabolic activity and require 25,000–100,000 cells/well and a full 4-hour incubation to achieve robust absorbance.

schedule Cell Line Tuning

calculate Absorbance Background Subtraction, Viability & ED50 Calculation

A_corrected = A_490(sample) - A_490(no-cell blank) Cell Viability (%) = [A_corrected(treated) / A_corrected(vehicle control)] × 100% ED_50 Response = [(A_max - A_min) / 2] + A_min

1. Net Absorbance Calculation:

$$A_{\text{corrected}} = A_{490\text{ (sample)}} - A_{490\text{ (no-cell control)}}$$

(Optional Dual-Wavelength Reference Correction: $A_{\text{net}} = [A_{490} - A_{650}]_{\text{sample}} - [A_{490} - A_{650}]_{\text{blank}}$)

2. Percent Relative Viability / Proliferation:

$$\text{Viability (\%)} = \frac{A_{\text{corrected(treated)}}}{A_{\text{corrected(vehicle control)}}} \times 100\%$$

3. Cytokine Bioassay $\text{ED}_{50}$ Determination:

Plot corrected $A_{490}$ against log cytokine/compound concentration. Fit a 4-parameter logistic sigmoidal curve. The $\text{ED}_{50}$ is the concentration corresponding to $50\%$ maximal biological proliferation response:

$$\text{Half-Maximal Absorbance} = \frac{A_{\max} - A_{\min}}{2} + A_{\min}$$

Storage & Stability

Store at –30°C to –10°C protected from light. For frequent use, store at +2°C to +10°C protected from light for up to 6 weeks.

event_available Stable until expiration date on label; performance maintained through 10 freeze-thaw cycles without loss of activity.

warning Cautions & Safety

  • check_circle CellTiter 96® AQueous One Solution Reagent is light-sensitive; store in amber bottles and minimize exposure to ambient laboratory light to prevent spontaneous reagent darkening and elevated baseline absorbance.
  • check_circle Strong chemical reducing agents (ascorbic acid, dithiothreitol, mercaptoethanol, glutathione) can reduce MTS non-enzymatically; always run compound-in-medium blank controls without cells.
  • check_circle Alkaline pH (> 7.8) accelerates spontaneous tetrazolium reduction; ensure culture medium is adequately buffered and equilibrated in 5% CO2.
  • check_circle Avoid introducing bubbles when pipetting reagent or mixing plates, as bubbles on the meniscus will distort spectrophotometric absorbance readings.
  • check_circle If using 10% SDS to stop the reaction, store plates in a humidified chamber protected from light at room temperature and read within 18 hours.
analytics

Expected Results

Generates a highly linear relationship between cell number and absorbance at 490 nm (correlation coefficient r > 0.99 from < 1,000 to > 100,000 cells/well) with minimal background absorbance (0.2–0.3 OD) and excellent reproducibility across replicates.
