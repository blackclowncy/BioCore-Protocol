Myeloperoxidase (MPO) Polyclonal Antibody

biotech Elabscience® • Catalog Number E-AB-70091 (60 µL / 120 µL / 200 µL)

info Overview & Principle

Myeloperoxidase (MPO) Polyclonal Antibody (Elabscience® Cat. No. E-AB-70091) is an antigen affinity-purified rabbit polyclonal antibody specifically engineered for high-sensitivity detection of mammalian myeloperoxidase in acute inflammation, tissue necrosis, neutrophil infiltration, and ischemia-reperfusion injury.

Enzyme Structure, Function & Diagnostic Value:

  • Neutrophil Granule Heme Peroxidase: MPO (EC 1.11.2.2) is a 140 kDa tetrameric heme enzyme consisting of two heavy chains (~59 kDa) and two light chains (~14 kDa). It is synthesized during myeloid differentiation and stored abundantly in the azurophilic (primary) granules of polymorphonuclear neutrophils (PMNs) and, to a lesser extent, in monocytes.
  • Oxidative Burst & NETosis Mediator: Catalyzes the conversion of hydrogen peroxide ($H_2O_2$) and chloride ($Cl^-$) into hypochlorous acid ($HOCl$), a potent microbicidal oxidant. Released into extracellular space and chromatin nets during NETosis (neutrophil extracellular trap formation).
  • Broad Cross-Species Reactivity: Demonstrates high reactivity in Human, Mouse, and Rat tissues. Validated in human liver, mouse bone marrow, human spleen, and post-ischemic reperfused organs.
  • Multi-Platform Validation: Optimized for Immunohistochemistry (IHC-P / IHC-F: 1:500–1:1000, 0.81–1.62 µg/mL) and Immunofluorescence (IF: 1:200–1:500, 1.62–4.05 µg/mL).

format_list_numbered Usage Procedure

Immunohistochemistry on Paraffin Sections for Neutrophil Detection

1

Deparaffinization & Rehydration

Deparaffinize FFPE sections in fresh xylene for 3 changes of 5 minutes each (3 × 5 min). Rehydrate through 100% ethanol (2 × 2 min), 95% ethanol (2 × 2 min), 75% ethanol (2 min), and rinse in distilled water for 2 minutes.

schedule 20 mins
2

Antigen Retrieval (HIER)

Place slides in a staining vessel containing 10 mM Sodium Citrate buffer (pH 6.0) or 1X Tris-EDTA buffer (pH 9.0). Microwave at high power for 2–3 minutes to bring to boil, then maintain sub-boiling (95–98°C) for 10–12 minutes. Allow slides to cool on the benchtop for 30 minutes. Rinse in 1X PBS for 2 × 2 minutes.

schedule 45 mins
3

Endogenous Peroxidase Inactivation & Serum Blocking

Immerse slides in 3% H2O2 in methanol (or 0.3% H2O2 in PBS) for 15 minutes at room temperature to eliminate endogenous neutrophil peroxidase activity. Rinse in PBS (2 × 2 min). Apply 5% normal goat serum in PBS for 30 minutes at room temperature in a humidified chamber.

infoCRITICAL: Because neutrophils express massive endogenous MPO, 3% H2O2 in methanol ensures complete background quenching.
schedule 45 mins
4

MPO Primary Antibody Incubation

Dilute MPO antibody stock (0.81 mg/mL) 1:500 to 1:1000 (0.81–1.62 µg/mL) in 1% BSA/PBS diluent. Apply 100–150 µL per section. Incubate for 1–2 hours at room temperature, or overnight at 4°C in a humidified chamber. Rinse in 1X PBS for 3 changes of 2 minutes (3 × 2 min).

schedule 1–2 hours (or O/N)
5

Polymer Secondary Detection & DAB Reaction

Apply polymer HRP-conjugated anti-rabbit secondary antibody for 30 minutes at room temperature. Rinse in 1X PBS for 3 × 2 minutes. Add fresh DAB working solution and monitor under a light microscope for 2–5 minutes until distinct brown cytoplasmic granular staining in neutrophils is evident. Stop reaction in ddH2O.

schedule 35 mins
6

Hematoxylin Counterstain & Permanent Mounting

Counterstain with Mayer's Hematoxylin for 1–2 minutes, blue in tap water for 2 minutes. Dehydrate through graded ethanol, clear in xylene (2 × 5 min), and mount with permanent coverslipping medium.

schedule 15 mins

Immunofluorescence Staining & NETosis Visualization

1

Sample Preparation & Fixation

For cultured neutrophils / NETosis assays, stimulate purified neutrophils with PMA (20–100 nM) or calcium ionophore for 2–4 hours on coverslips. Fix cells in 4% PFA in PBS for 15 minutes at room temperature. For tissue cryosections, fix in 4% PFA for 10 minutes. Rinse in PBS (2 × 2 min).

schedule 20 mins
2

Permeabilization & Blocking

Permeabilize in 0.2% Triton X-100 in PBS for 10 minutes at room temperature. Block non-specific binding with 5% BSA + 5% normal goat serum in PBS for 1 hour at room temperature.

schedule 70 mins
3

Dual Primary Antibody Incubation (MPO + CitH3)

Dilute MPO antibody 1:200–1:500 (1.62–4.05 µg/mL) in 1% BSA/PBS. If co-staining for NETs, include mouse anti-Citrullinated Histone H3 (CitH3, 1:200). Incubate overnight at 4°C in a dark humidified chamber. Wash with PBS-T (3 × 5 min).

schedule Overnight at 4°C
4

Fluorescent Detection & Confocal Imaging

Incubate with Goat anti-Rabbit IgG Alexa Fluor 488 (green, for MPO) and Goat anti-Mouse IgG Alexa Fluor 594 (red, for CitH3) at 2 µg/mL for 1 hour at room temperature in the dark. Wash 3 × 5 min. Counterstain with DAPI (1 µg/mL) for 5 minutes. Mount with antifade mounting medium.

infoExtracellular web-like co-localization of MPO, CitH3, and DAPI confirms active NETosis.
schedule 90 mins

Cryosection Processing & Rapid MPO Detection

1

Cryosection Preparation

Cut 5–10 µm cryosections from OCT-embedded fresh-frozen biopsy specimens (e.g. liver, kidney, myocardium). Mount onto Superfrost Plus slides and air dry for 30 minutes at room temperature.

schedule 30 mins
2

Acetone Fixation & Endogenous Quench

Fix in ice-cold acetone for 10 minutes at 4°C. Air dry for 15 minutes. Wash in 1X PBS for 2 × 2 minutes. Quench endogenous peroxidases in 0.3% H2O2/PBS for 10 minutes at room temperature. Rinse in PBS.

schedule 35 mins
3

Staining & Chromogenic / Fluorescent Detection

Block with 5% normal goat serum for 30 minutes. Incubate with MPO antibody (1:500 dilution) for 1 hour at room temperature. Detect with anti-rabbit polymer HRP + DAB or Alexa Fluor 488 secondary conjugate.

schedule 90 mins

Neutrophil Density Quantification & Infiltration Index

1

Low-Power Parenchymal Survey

Scan the entire tissue section under 100× magnification to evaluate overall inflammatory distribution and identify ischemic or necrotic foci.

schedule 5 mins
2

High-Power Field (400× HPF) Counting

Count distinct MPO-positive neutrophils (characterized by dark brown multi-lobed or granular staining) across 5 to 10 randomly selected high-power fields (400×, 0.2 mm² field diameter).

schedule 15 mins
3

Semi-Quantitative Infiltration Scoring Matrix

• Grade 0 (Physiological / Normal): 0–2 MPO⁺ cells / HPF (isolated resident cells) • Grade 1 (Mild Infiltration): 3–5 MPO⁺ cells / HPF • Grade 2 (Moderate Infiltration): 6–20 MPO⁺ cells / HPF (sinusoidal plugging / interstitial recruitment) • Grade 3 (Severe / Necrotic): > 20 MPO⁺ cells / HPF (dense neutrophilic aggregates, abscess formation, or massive reperfusion injury).

schedule Grading

Controls & Optimization Guide (E-AB-70091)

1

Positive & Negative Quality Controls

• Positive Control: Human or mouse spleen red pulp, bone marrow, tonsil, or acute inflammatory liver / lung tissue. • Negative Control: Normal rabbit IgG isotype control at equivalent concentration, or omission of primary antibody.

schedule QC Standard
2

Troubleshooting: Diffuse Non-Specific Background

• Cause 1: Inadequate endogenous peroxidase quench. Neutrophils contain massive endogenous peroxidase; weak 0.3% H2O2 in water may be insufficient. -> Remedy: Use 3% H2O2 in reagent-grade methanol for 15–20 minutes. • Cause 2: Over-incubation of DAB substrate. -> Remedy: Reduce DAB development to 2–3 minutes and monitor under microscope. • Cause 3: High non-specific protein binding. -> Remedy: Increase BSA to 3% and include 5% normal goat serum in blocking buffer.

schedule Remedy
3

Troubleshooting: Weak Neutrophil Signal

• Cause 1: Over-fixation in formalin (>48h) masking epitopes. -> Remedy: Optimize HIER using pH 9.0 Tris-EDTA buffer at 95–98°C for 15 minutes. • Cause 2: Inappropriate dilution. -> Remedy: Titrate antibody from 1:200 to 1:500.

schedule Remedy

warning Cautions & Safety

  • check_circle Store antibody stock vial at -20°C. Formulated with 50% glycerol and remains liquid at -20°C; do not store in frost-free freezers to prevent temperature fluctuations.
  • check_circle Neutrophils possess massive endogenous peroxidase activity; quenching with 3% H2O2 in methanol is essential in IHC to prevent false-positive non-specific staining.
  • check_circle Handle DAB substrate with extreme care in a fume hood; dispose in 10% bleach hazardous waste.
  • check_circle Always maintain hydration of tissue sections in a humidified chamber during antibody incubation.

Expected Results

High-contrast dark brown (IHC) or brilliant green/red fluorescent (IF) cytoplasmic granular staining in polymorphonuclear neutrophils, clearly delineating acute inflammatory recruitment and NETosis.
