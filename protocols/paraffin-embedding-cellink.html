Paraffin Embedding of Tissue Samples

biotech Bridges Lab (University of Michigan) • UMich Bridges Lab Protocols

info Overview & Principle

The Paraffin Embedding of Tissue Samples Protocol (from the Bridges Lab, University of Michigan School of Public Health) details the complete procedural workflow for standard laboratory processing, fixation, graded alcohol dehydration, clearing with Citrosolv, molten paraffin infiltration, and precision block casting of animal and human tissue specimens.

Primary Purpose: Thorough dehydration and wax infiltration of formalin-fixed specimens to create robust, rigid paraffin blocks suitable for microtome sectioning (4–7 µm), histological stains (H&E, PAS, Trichrome), and immunohistochemical (IHC) staining.

Associated Institutional Safety Guidelines: SOP - Carcinogens • SOP - Flammables and Combustibles • SOP - Formalin and Paraformaldehyde • SOP - Irritants.

timer Timing Strategy: Dehydration and infiltration involve multiple long incubation cycles (~10–12 hours total). This protocol should be initiated first thing in the morning unless overnight holding at permissible stages (such as the 3rd paraffin incubation or 70% EtOH storage) is planned.

format_list_numbered Usage Procedure

Bridges Lab 14-Step Complete Paraffin Embedding Workflow

1

1. Tissue Harvest & Formalin Fixation

Harvest tissue specimen from animal, place immediately into a labelled biopsy/tissue cassette, and submerge in 10% neutral buffered formalin (NBF) for 24–48 hours (depending on tissue sample thickness and density).

schedule 24–48 hours
2

2. Transfer to 70% Ethanol & Staging

Move cassettes into 70% ethanol for 24 hours. • Note: Samples can be safely left in 70% ethanol for long-term storage if required. • Core Facility Option: At this step, samples can alternatively be submitted to the Rogel Cancer Center's Tissue and Molecular Pathology Core for automated machine processing. • Planning Warning: Subsequent dehydration steps involve many long incubation periods and must be started first thing in the morning.

schedule 24 hours (or long-term)
3

3. Wax Pot Initialization

Turn on wax pots and preheat histological paraffin in beakers to 58–60°C (the melting point of paraffin is 58°C; maintaining temperature 2°C above melting point yields optimal penetration).

schedule Pre-heating
4

4. 75% Ethanol Incubation

Move cassettes into 75% ethanol and incubate for 30 minutes at room temperature.

schedule 30 mins
5

5. 95% Ethanol Dehydration (2 Cycles)

Move cassettes into fresh 95% ethanol for 75 minutes. Repeat this step a second time using a fresh container of 95% ethanol (Total: 2 × 75 min = 150 min).

schedule 2 × 75 mins (2.5 hrs)
6

6. 100% Anhydrous Ethanol Dehydration (3 Cycles)

Move cassettes into 100% anhydrous ethanol for 60 minutes. Repeat this step twice more using fresh 100% ethanol each time (Total: 3 × 60 min = 3 hrs). • Note: Some protocols allow the first 100% ethanol wash to sit overnight if scheduling requires, but extended dehydration can alter tissue micro-morphology. • During these incubations, prepare beakers of melted 58–60°C paraffin.

schedule 3 × 60 mins (3 hrs)
7

7. Citrosolv Clearing (2 Cycles)

Move cassettes into Citrosolv (clearing agent) for 30–60 minutes inside a chemical fume hood. Repeat this step a second time using fresh Citrosolv (Total: 2 × 30–60 min). Tissues will become cleared and translucent.

schedule 2 × 30–60 mins
8

8. 60°C Molten Paraffin Infiltration (3 Cycles)

Move cassettes into 60°C melted paraffin for 60 minutes. Repeat this step twice more using fresh paraffin beakers each time (Total: 3 × 60 min). • Note: The 2nd or 3rd paraffin incubation can optionally be extended overnight, but excessive hot wax exposure may increase tissue brittleness during microtomy. • During these steps, turn on the paraffin embedding station (Link Building, Room 311).

schedule 3 × 60 mins (or O/N)
9

9. Mold Preparation & Base Layer

Immediately prior to embedding, lightly spray the metallic base mold with 'Mold Grease' release spray. Dispense a shallow foundation layer of melted paraffin into the bottom of the mold.

schedule ~2 mins
10

10. Specimen Orientation

Open the cassette, remove the tissue with warm forceps, and position the specimen flat or on-edge (for cross-sectional examination) on top of the base wax. Partially cover with additional molten paraffin.

schedule ~2 mins
11

11. Cassette Placement & Mold Filling

Place the labelled pathology cassette base directly on top of the base mold, and completely fill the mold and cassette reservoir with molten paraffin wax.

schedule ~2 mins
12

12. Cold Plate Solidification

Transfer the filled mold assembly onto the 'Cold Side' (cooling plate) of the embedding station. The paraffin block will solidify and contract away from the mold within 10–15 minutes.

schedule 10–15 mins
13

13. Block Demolding

Remove the solidified paraffin block from the metal mold (with proper mold grease and contraction, it slips out easily).

schedule ~1 min
14

14. Block Archival & Storage

Trim excess flash paraffin around the edges of the cassette. Store blocks at room temperature until ready for rotary microtome sectioning (4–7 µm).

schedule Indefinite storage

Storage & Stability

Store reagents (Formalin, Ethanol series, Citrosolv, Paraffin) at room temperature in flammable/chemical storage cabinets.

event_available Paraffin-embedded tissue blocks are stable indefinitely when stored at room temperature.

warning Cautions & Safety

  • check_circle Always handle formalin, 100% ethanol, and Citrosolv inside a certified chemical fume hood with proper PPE (nitrile gloves, lab coat, eye protection).
  • check_circle Do not overheat paraffin above 60–62°C; higher temperatures denature tissue antigens and make wax brittle.
  • check_circle Start the dehydration series early in the morning due to the 11+ hours of sequential incubation steps.
  • check_circle Avoid over-prolonged exposure to hot paraffin wax (longer than overnight) to prevent severe tissue hardening and blade chatter during microtomy.
  • check_circle Ensure 'Mold Grease' is applied sparingly to base molds to facilitate clean release without contaminating the specimen face.
analytics

Expected Results

Flawlessly oriented, bubble-free paraffin-embedded tissue blocks with intact cellular architecture, ready for microtome sectioning at 4–7 µm and downstream H&E, IHC, or special histochemical staining.
