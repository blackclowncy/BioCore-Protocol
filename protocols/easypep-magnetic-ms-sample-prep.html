EasyPep™ Magnetic MS Sample Prep Kit

biotech Thermo Scientific • Catalog Number A57867 (96 reactions)

info Overview & Principle

The Thermo Scientific™ EasyPep™ Magnetic MS Sample Prep Kit enables rapid, robust, and highly reproducible processing of cultured mammalian cells, tissues, and plasma for proteomic liquid chromatography-mass spectrometry (LC-MS) analysis. The protocol takes less than 3 hours from raw sample to MS-ready peptides.

Workflow Highlights: Includes Universal Nuclease for rapid DNA/RNA digestion without sonication, a 'one-pot' reduction and alkylation reagent (+57.02 carbamidomethylation), an optimized MS-grade Trypsin/Lys-C protease mix, and magnetic clean-up beads eliminating the need for SpeedVac drying.

format_list_numbered Usage Procedure

96-Reaction Magnetic MS Sample Preparation Protocol

1

Cell Lysis & Nuclease Treatment

Add 100 µL Lysis Buffer + 1 µL Universal Nuclease to 15–100 µg protein equivalent of cell pellet or tissue homogenate. Pipette up and down to lyse and clarify.

schedule 10 mins
2

Reduction & Alkylation (One-Pot)

Add 10 µL Reduction Solution and 10 µL Alkylation Solution to each lysate. Incubate on a thermal mixer at 95°C for 10 minutes (or 50°C for 20 mins).

infoSimultaneous reduction and carbamidomethylation in single reaction.
schedule 10 mins
3

Enzymatic Digestion

Add reconstituted Trypsin/Lys-C enzyme mix (1:40 to 1:20 enzyme-to-protein ratio). Incubate with shaking at 37°C for 1–2 hours.

schedule 60–120 mins
4

Magnetic Bead Binding & Clean-Up

Add Digestion Stop Solution and 50 µL Magnetic Beads. Bind for 5 mins, place on magnetic stand, wash twice with Wash Solution to remove salts/detergents.

schedule 15 mins
5

Elution & Direct LC-MS Loading

Add 50–100 µL Elution Buffer, mix for 5 mins, place on magnet, and transfer clear supernatant. Sample is immediately ready for LC-MS or TMT labeling.

infoNo vacuum centrifugation or peptide dry-down required.
schedule 10 mins

Storage & Stability

Store kit components as indicated: Enzyme mix and beads at 4°C, Lysis/Digestion buffers at RT or -20°C.

event_available Stable for 1 year when stored at recommended temperatures.

warning Cautions & Safety

  • check_circle Do not freeze Magnetic Clean-up Beads; irreversible bead aggregation will reduce peptide recovery.
  • check_circle Use MS-grade low-binding tubes and pipette tips to prevent plasticizer polymer contamination in mass spectrometer.
  • check_circle Ensure complete magnetic separation (30–60 sec) before aspirating supernatant during wash steps.
analytics

Expected Results

High peptide recovery (>80%) with >95% cleavage efficiency, high missed-cleavage fidelity (<5%), and complete removal of MS-interfering detergents.
