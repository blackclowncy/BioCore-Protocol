EasyPep™ Magnetic MS Sample Prep Kit

biotech Thermo Scientific • Catalog Number A57867 (96 reactions)

info Overview & Principle

The Thermo Scientific™ EasyPep™ Magnetic MS Sample Prep Kit (Cat. No. A57867, 96 Reactions, Doc. MAN0030132 Rev. B) enables rapid, robust, and reproducible processing of cultured mammalian cells, plasma, serum, and tissues for proteomic liquid chromatography-mass spectrometry (LC-MS) analysis. Optimized to process protein samples from 15–100 µg (and scalable up to 1500 µg) with high peptide yield and purity.

Key Technical Innovations:

  • Total Protocol Duration < 3 Hours: Replaces traditional overnight digestion and eliminates vacuum drying/SpeedVac reconstitution.
  • Universal Nuclease Treatment: Rapidly degrades genomic DNA/RNA viscosity within minutes without requiring sonicators.
  • 'One-Pot' Reduction & Alkylation: Simultaneous disulfide reduction and cysteine carbamidomethylation (+57.02 Da) at 95°C (5–10 min) or 50°C (20–30 min).
  • MS-Grade Trypsin/Lys-C Protease Mix: High-cleavage fidelity with <5% missed cleavage rates.
  • Magnetic Clean-up Beads: Direct solid-phase peptide purification; eluted peptides are ready for direct LC-MS injection or TMT™/TMTpro™ isobaric labeling.
  • Automation Compatible: Validated for high-throughput automated processing on KingFisher™ Apex and KingFisher™ Flex systems.

format_list_numbered Usage Procedure

Sample Extraction, Lysis & Protein Normalization

1

PBS Pre-Wash

Rinse cultured cells or tissues 2–3 times with 1× PBS to remove culture media, FBS, or residual blood before adding Lysis Solution. (Resuspend samples in Lysis Solution without additional buffers).

schedule 5 mins
2

Cultured Mammalian Cell Lysis

Add 25 µL Lysis Solution and 1 µL Universal Nuclease to a minimum of 1 × 10⁶ cells. Pipet up and down (with a P200 tip) 10–15 times until sample viscosity is fully reduced. (Centrifugation of cell lysates is typically NOT required).

infoWarm Lysis Solution to room temperature before use.
schedule 10 mins
3

Tissue Homogenization

Add 25 µL Lysis Solution (containing 1 µL Universal Nuclease) per 5 mg of tissue. Disrupt with a tissue homogenizer until completely homogenized. Centrifuge tissue lysates at 16,000 × g for 10 minutes at room temperature, then transfer supernatant.

schedule 15 mins
4

Serum, Plasma & Purified Proteins

Dilute purified protein, serum, or plasma samples directly in Lysis Solution to a concentration of 2–5 µg/µL. (Note: Universal Nuclease is NOT required for serum, plasma, or purified proteins).

schedule 5 mins
5

Protein Quantitation (BCA)

Determine protein concentration using Pierce™ BCA Protein Assay (Cat. 23227) or Rapid Gold BCA (Cat. A53226). Adjust sample concentration to 2–5 µg/µL using Lysis Solution. Transfer 15–100 µg into low-protein-binding microcentrifuge tubes or 96-deep-well plates.

infoProtein sample concentration must be ≥2–5 µg/µL to ensure digest concentration is ≥0.06 µg/µL for magnetic binding.
schedule 20 mins

One-Pot Reduction, Alkylation & Trypsin/Lys-C Proteolysis

1

Add Reduction Solution

Add the appropriate volume of Reduction Solution according to Table 1 (7.5 µL for 15–30 µg; 15 µL for 30–60 µg; 30 µL for 60–100 µg; see Table 1) and briefly vortex.

schedule 2 mins
2

Add Alkylation Solution

Add the appropriate volume of Alkylation Solution according to Table 1 (3.75 µL for 15–30 µg; 7.5 µL for 30–60 µg; 15 µL for 60–100 µg) and briefly vortex.

schedule 2 mins
3

One-Pot Reduction & Alkylation Incubation

Incubate sample at 95°C on a heat block/thermal mixer for 5–10 minutes, OR at 50°C for 20–30 minutes to reduce and alkylate cysteines (+57.02 Da).

infoAllow sample to cool to room temperature. Briefly centrifuge to collect condensation.
schedule 5–10 mins
4

Reconstitute Trypsin/Lys-C Protease Mix

Add 150 µL Enzyme Reconstitution Solution to 1 vial (100 µg) of Pierce™ Trypsin/Lys-C Protease Mix. Mix gently.

infoReconstituted enzyme is stable at 4°C for 1 month or -20°C for 1 year.
schedule 3 mins
5

Add Enzyme & Digest

Add appropriate volume of reconstituted enzyme (Table 1: 3.75 µL for 15–30 µg; 7.5 µL for 30–100 µg). Incubate with shaking at 37°C for 1–2 hours. (For 60–100 µg input, 2–3 hours shaking is recommended).

infoDigests can be labeled with TMT™ reagents at this point before clean-up if desired.
schedule 1–3 hrs

Manual Solid-Phase Magnetic Peptide Clean-Up Protocol

1

Condition & Rinse Magnetic Clean-up Beads

Vortex Magnetic Clean-Up Beads thoroughly to fully resuspend. Transfer 20 µL beads (for 15–100 µg) to a low-protein-binding tube/plate. Collect beads on magnetic stand, remove storage buffer. Remove from magnet, add 200 µL Bind Solution (Table 1 [2]), vortex, collect on magnet, and discard supernatant.

schedule 5 mins
2

Bind Digested Peptides to Beads

Add Table 1 Bind Solution [3] (230 µL for 15–30 µg; 460 µL for 30–60 µg; 920 µL for 60–100 µg) to rinsed beads. Transfer bead slurry into the protein digest tube. Incubate at RT for 30–60 minutes with shaking at 1,200 rpm on a thermomixer.

infoProtein digest must be diluted in Bind Solution by >90% by volume before binding.
schedule 30–60 mins
3

Wash Bound Peptides

Collect beads on magnetic stand, discard supernatant. Remove from magnet, add 500 µL Wash Solution (Table 1), vortex briefly, collect on magnet, and discard wash supernatant. (For TMT-labeled samples, wash twice using 250 µL each).

schedule 5 mins
4

Elute MS-Ready Peptides

Add designated volume of Elution Solution (30 µL for 15–30 µg; 90 µL for 30–60 µg; 150 µL for 60–100 µg). Vortex briefly and incubate for 3 minutes while mixing on a thermomixer at room temperature.

schedule 3 mins
5

Magnetic Separation & Autosampler Transfer

Collect beads on magnetic stand. Transfer eluted peptide solution to new low-binding tube or autosampler vial. Centrifuge at 10,000 × g for 2 minutes before LC-MS injection to avoid any bead carryover.

infoSamples are ready for direct LC-MS injection without vacuum evaporation!
schedule 5 mins

Automated High-Throughput Clean-Up (KingFisher™ Apex / Flex)

1

Load Instrument Script

Select KingFisher script: EasyPep_Magnetic_Kit_15-30ug.kfx, EasyPep_Magnetic_Kit_30-60ug.kfx, or EasyPep_Magnetic_Kit_60-100ug.kfx on KingFisher Apex/Flex instrument.

schedule 2 mins
2

Plate 1 (Tip Plate)

Combine 96 Deep-Well Tip Comb (Cat. 97002534) with a KingFisher 96 Deep-Well Plate.

schedule 1 min
3

Plate 2 (Bead Plate)

Dispense 100 µL total slurry per well (20 µL Magnetic Clean-up Beads diluted in 80 µL Bind Solution).

schedule 5 mins
4

Plate 3 (Bead Wash Plate)

Dispense 200 µL Bind Solution per well (Mix: 1 minute, Medium speed).

schedule 3 mins
5

Plate 4 (Sample Binding Plate)

Load protein digest in Bind Solution: 250 µL (for 15–30 µg digest), 500 µL (for 30–60 µg digest), or 1000 µL (for 60–100 µg digest). (Mix: 40 minutes, Medium speed).

schedule 40 mins
6

Plate 5 (Wash Plate)

Dispense 500 µL Wash Solution per well (Mix: 2 minutes, Medium speed).

schedule 2 mins
7

Plate 6 (Elution Plate)

Dispense 50 µL–150 µL Elution Solution per well (Mix: 3 minutes, Medium speed). Collect eluted peptides for analysis.

schedule 3 mins

TMT™ / TMTpro™ Isobaric Tag Labeling Protocols

1

Dissolve Reagents & Sample Ratios

Dissolve TMT™ / TMTpro™ reagents in 100% anhydrous acetonitrile. For 1–10 µg digests: use 1:16 (TMT) or 1:20 (TMTpro) w:w. For 10–100 µg digests: use 1:4–1:8 (TMT) or 1:5–1:10 (TMTpro) w:w.

schedule 5 mins
2

Option A: Pre-Clean-Up Labeling Workflow

Add dissolved TMT reagent directly to uncleaned digest. Incubate 30–60 mins at RT. Quench with 5 µL of 5% hydroxylamine / 20% formic acid solution for 5 mins. Pool equal amounts, then proceed to Table 1 Manual Magnetic Clean-Up based on total pooled mass.

schedule 45 mins
3

Option B: Post-Clean-Up Labeling Workflow

Dry cleaned peptides in SpeedVac, resuspend in 100 mM TEAB (pH 8.5) or HEPES (pH 8.0). Add TMT reagent, incubate 30–60 mins at RT, quench with 5 µL 5% hydroxylamine for 5 mins. Pool, acidify with 5% TFA until pH < 3, and desalt with Pierce Peptide Desalting Spin Columns (Cat. 89852).

schedule 60 mins

Troubleshooting & Optimization Guide (MAN0030132)

1

High Sample Viscosity after Lysis

Cause: Universal Nuclease omitted or inhibited by EDTA in protease inhibitors. Remedy: Add 1 µL Universal Nuclease per 25–100 µL lysis buffer. Never add protease inhibitor cocktails containing EDTA.

schedule Immediate
2

Incomplete Digestion

Cause: Enzyme added before cooling sample, or insufficient incubation. Remedy: Cool samples to RT after reduction/alkylation before adding Trypsin/Lys-C. Increase digestion time to 3 hours with shaking at 37°C.

schedule Adjustment
3

Low Peptide Yield

Cause: Protein concentration too dilute (<2 µg/µL) or digest not diluted >90% by volume in Bind Solution. Remedy: Verify initial concentration is 2–5 µg/µL. Follow exact Table 1 Bind Solution volumes.

schedule Adjustment
4

Bead Carryover in Elution

Cause: Incomplete magnetic capture before pipetting. Remedy: Centrifuge eluted peptide samples at 10,000 × g for 2 minutes before transferring to autosampler vials, or use C18 trap columns.

schedule Adjustment

Storage & Stability

Store kit components as indicated: Enzyme mix and beads at 4°C, Lysis/Digestion buffers at RT or -20°C.

event_available Stable for 1 year when stored at recommended temperatures.

warning Cautions & Safety

  • check_circle Never freeze Magnetic Clean-up Beads; irreversible bead aggregation will destroy peptide recovery.
  • check_circle Do not add EDTA-containing protease inhibitor cocktails as EDTA inhibits Universal Nuclease and Trypsin/Lys-C.
  • check_circle Cool samples to room temperature after 95°C reduction/alkylation before adding Trypsin/Lys-C protease.
  • check_circle Centrifuge eluted peptides at 10,000 × g for 2 minutes before transferring to autosampler vials to prevent bead carryover.
analytics

Expected Results

High peptide recovery (>80%) with >95% cleavage efficiency (<5% missed cleavages), complete removal of MS-interfering detergents and salts, and direct compatibility with nano-LC-MS/MS or TMT labeling.
