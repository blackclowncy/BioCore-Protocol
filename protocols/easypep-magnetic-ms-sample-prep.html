EasyPep™ Magnetic MS Sample Prep Kit

biotech Thermo Scientific • Catalog Number A57867 (96 reactions)

info Overview & Principle

The EasyPep™ Magnetic MS Sample Prep Kit (Thermo Fisher Pub. No. MAN0030132 Rev. B, Cat. No. A57867) provides a rapid, robust magnetic bead-based workflow for processing cultured cells, mammalian tissues, plasma, and serum into clean peptide digests ready for LC-MS/MS and TMT™ isobaric labeling in less than 3 hours.

Key Advantages & Chemistry:

  • All-in-One Lysis & DNA/RNA Clearance: High-efficiency lysis buffer supplemented with Universal Nuclease rapidly clears viscosity in minutes without shear loss or centrifugation steps.
  • 10-Minute Single-Tube Reduction & Alkylation: High-temperature ($95^\circ\text{C}$ for 5–10 min) rapid chemical denaturation, disulfide reduction, and cysteine alkylation (Carbamidomethylation, +57.02 Da).
  • Optimized MS-Grade Trypsin/Lys-C Protease Mix: Reconstituted in specialized Enzyme Reconstitution Solution to drive rapid, highly specific cleavage at $37^\circ\text{C}$ in 1–3 hours.
  • Magnetic Bead Clean-up & Desalting: Replaces traditional spin columns with magnetic beads, eliminating detergents, salts, and unreacted reagents with recovery rates > 85%. Fully compatible with automated KingFisher™ platforms.

format_list_numbered Usage Procedure

Cell, Tissue, and Biofluid Lysis Protocols

1

Adherent and Suspension Cultured Cells

Pellet cells at 300 × g for 5 minutes and wash with 1X PBS. For ≥ 1 × 10⁶ cells, add 25 µL Lysis Solution and 1 µL Universal Nuclease per sample. Pipette up and down 10–15 times using a P200 pipette to break up cell aggregates and reduce viscosity. Do NOT centrifuge; proceed directly with whole lysate.

infoNuclease rapidly hydrolyzes DNA and RNA, eliminating viscosity without probe sonication.
schedule 10 mins
2

Solid Mammalian Tissues

Weigh frozen or fresh tissue (5–20 mg). Add 25 µL Lysis Solution and 1 µL Universal Nuclease per 5 mg tissue. Homogenize with a motorized pestle or bead mill on ice. Centrifuge lysate at 16,000 × g for 10 minutes at 4°C to pellet insoluble cellular debris. Transfer clear supernatant to a new tube.

schedule 20 mins
3

Plasma, Serum, and Purified Protein Solutions

Dilute plasma, serum, or purified proteins in Lysis Solution to achieve a final protein concentration of 2.0–5.0 µg/µL. (Universal Nuclease is not required for serum or purified proteins).

schedule 5 mins
4

Protein Quantification (BCA Assay)

Determine total protein concentration using the Pierce™ BCA Protein Assay Kit (Cat. 23225) or Rapid Gold BCA. Normalize protein concentration to 2–5 µg/µL in Lysis Solution.

infoMaintaining initial protein concentration between 2–5 µg/µL ensures optimal binding kinetics.
schedule 30 mins

Single-Tube Reduction, Alkylation & Enzymatic Digestion

1

Reagent Addition for Reduction and Alkylation

Aliquot protein sample (e.g. 100 µg in 30 µL Lysis Solution) into a 1.5 mL low-protein-binding microcentrifuge tube or 96-well deep-well plate. Add Reduction Solution (15 µL) and Alkylation Solution (15 µL) according to Table 1.

schedule 5 mins
2

Thermal Denaturation & Modification

Incubate sample at 95°C for 5–10 minutes using a thermal cycler or dry heat block (or alternatively at 50°C for 20–30 minutes). Allow sample to cool to room temperature (18–25°C) for 3–5 minutes.

infoCooling to room temperature is critical before adding enzymes to prevent protease denaturation.
schedule 10 mins
3

Enzyme Reconstitution (Trypsin/Lys-C)

Reconstitute 1 vial (100 µg) of Trypsin/Lys-C Protease Mix with 150 µL of Enzyme Reconstitution Solution. Gently swirl to dissolve completely.

infoReconstituted enzyme can be stored at -20°C for up to 1 month.
schedule 5 mins
4

Enzyme Addition & Digestion

Add reconstituted Trypsin/Lys-C enzyme mix (e.g. 7.5 µL for 60–100 µg protein) according to Table 1. Mix thoroughly. Incubate at 37°C with shaking (300–500 rpm) for 1–3 hours (or 2–3 hours for 60–100 µg protein samples).

schedule 1–3 hours
5

Digestion Termination

Add 7.5 µL of Digestion Stop Solution to terminate protease activity. Mix by gentle vortexing.

schedule 2 mins

Manual Magnetic Bead Peptide Purification Protocol

1

Bead Pre-washing & Equilibration

Vortex Peptide Clean-up Beads thoroughly to resuspend. Transfer 20 µL beads (for 15–100 µg protein) into a 1.5 mL tube or 96-well plate. Add 200 µL Peptide Bind Solution [2]. Mix by pipetting 10 times. Place on magnetic stand for 1–2 minutes until solution clears; discard supernatant.

schedule 5 mins
2

Peptide Binding

Add Peptide Bind Solution [3] (920 µL for 100 µg sample) directly to the digested sample tube. Mix and transfer the entire mixture to the tube containing the washed beads. Mix by pipetting 10 times.

schedule 5 mins
3

Binding Incubation

Incubate sample-bead mix on a thermomixer or orbital shaker at 1,200 rpm for 30–60 minutes at room temperature (20–25°C).

schedule 30–60 mins
4

Magnetic Capture & Supernatant Removal

Place tube on magnetic stand for 2 minutes until beads form a tight pellet. Carefully aspirate and discard supernatant without disturbing beads.

schedule 2 mins
5

Peptide Wash Step

Remove tube from magnet. Add 500 µL Peptide Wash Solution. Mix on thermomixer at 1,200 rpm for 2 minutes (or pipette 15 times). Place on magnet for 2 minutes; discard supernatant. Repeat wash for a total of 2 washes.

schedule 8 mins
6

Peptide Elution

Add 150 µL Peptide Elution Solution to the beads. Mix on thermomixer at 1,200 rpm for 3 minutes at room temperature. Place on magnet for 2 minutes. Transfer clear supernatant containing purified peptides to a fresh low-binding tube or autosampler vial.

schedule 5 mins
7

Centrifugation for Bead Carryover Elimination

Centrifuge eluted peptide samples at 10,000 × g for 2 minutes to pellet any residual magnetic beads before injecting into LC-MS or SpeedVac drying.

schedule 2 mins

KingFisher™ Apex / Flex 96 Deep-Well Automated Protocol

1

Plate 1 (Tip Comb Plate)

Place 96 deep-well tip comb into a standard 96 deep-well plate.

schedule 1 min
2

Plate 2 (Bead Plate)

Dispense 100 µL Peptide Clean-up Beads per well.

schedule 2 mins
3

Plate 3 (Bead Wash Plate)

Dispense 200 µL Peptide Bind Solution per well (Mix: 1 minute, Slow speed).

schedule 2 mins
4

Plate 4 (Sample Binding Plate)

Combine digested sample with Peptide Bind Solution [3] (250–1000 µL total volume). (Mix: 40 minutes, Medium speed).

schedule 40 mins
5

Plate 5 (Wash Plate)

Dispense 500 µL Peptide Wash Solution per well (Mix: 2 minutes, Fast speed).

schedule 2 mins
6

Plate 6 (Elution Plate)

Dispense 50 µL–150 µL Elution Solution per well (Mix: 3 minutes, Medium speed). Collect eluted peptides for analysis.

schedule 3 mins

TMT™ / TMTpro™ Isobaric Tag Labeling Protocols

1

Dissolve Reagents & Sample Ratios

Dissolve TMT™ / TMTpro™ reagents in 100% anhydrous acetonitrile. For 1–10 µg digests: use 1:16 (TMT) or 1:20 (TMTpro) w:w. For 10–100 µg digests: use 1:4–1:8 (TMT) or 1:5–1:10 (TMTpro) w:w.

schedule 5 mins
2

Option A: Pre-Clean-Up Labeling Workflow

Add dissolved TMT reagent directly to uncleaned digest. Incubate 30–60 mins at RT. Quench with 5 µL of 5% hydroxylamine / 20% formic acid solution for 5 mins. Pool equal amounts, then proceed to Table 1 Manual Magnetic Clean-Up based on total pooled mass.

schedule 45 mins
3

Option B: Post-Clean-Up Labeling Workflow

Dry cleaned peptides in SpeedVac, resuspend in 100 mM TEAB (pH 8.5) or HEPES (pH 8.0). Add TMT reagent, incubate 30–60 mins at RT, quench with 5 µL 5% hydroxylamine for 5 mins. Pool, acidify with 5% TFA until pH < 3, and desalt with Pierce Peptide Desalting Spin Columns (Cat. 89852).

schedule 60 mins

Troubleshooting & Optimization Guide (MAN0030132)

1

High Sample Viscosity after Lysis

Cause: Universal Nuclease omitted or inhibited by EDTA in protease inhibitors. Remedy: Add 1 µL Universal Nuclease per 25–100 µL lysis buffer. Never add protease inhibitor cocktails containing EDTA.

schedule Immediate
2

Incomplete Digestion

Cause: Enzyme added before cooling sample, or insufficient incubation. Remedy: Cool samples to RT after reduction/alkylation before adding Trypsin/Lys-C. Increase digestion time to 3 hours with shaking at 37°C.

schedule Adjustment
3

Low Peptide Yield

Cause: Protein concentration too dilute (<2 µg/µL) or digest not diluted >90% by volume in Bind Solution. Remedy: Verify initial concentration is 2–5 µg/µL. Follow exact Table 1 Bind Solution volumes.

schedule Adjustment
4

Bead Carryover in Elution

Cause: Incomplete magnetic capture before pipetting. Remedy: Centrifuge eluted peptide samples at 10,000 × g for 2 minutes before transferring to autosampler vials, or use C18 trap columns.

schedule Adjustment

Storage & Stability

Store kit components as indicated: Enzyme mix and beads at 4°C, Lysis/Digestion buffers at RT or -20°C.

event_available Stable for 1 year when stored at recommended temperatures.

warning Cautions & Safety

  • check_circle Do NOT freeze the Peptide Clean-up Beads; irreversible clumping and complete loss of binding capacity will occur.
  • check_circle Do not use protease inhibitors containing EDTA, as EDTA inhibits Universal Nuclease.
  • check_circle Ensure digested sample is cooled to room temperature before adding enzyme or binding solution.
  • check_circle Centrifuge eluted peptides at 10,000 × g for 2 min before LC-MS injection to prevent autosampler needle clog.
analytics

Expected Results

Clean, highly reproducible peptide recovery > 85% with > 95% digestion efficiency (missed cleavages < 5%) and full compatibility with direct LC-MS/MS or TMT multiplexing.
