γ-Glutamyl Transferase (GGT/γ-GT) Activity Assay Kit

biotech Invitrogen / Thermo Fisher Scientific • Catalog Number EEA029 (96 tests)

info Overview & Principle

γ-Glutamyl Transferase (GGT / γ-GT) is a membrane-bound glycoprotein enzyme that catalyzes the transfer of γ-glutamyl groups from peptides like glutathione to other amino acids. It is highly abundant in the hepatobiliary tract, renal brush border, and pancreas.

Assay Principle: GGT catalyzes the transfer of the glutamyl moiety from L-γ-glutamyl-3-carboxy-4-nitroanilide (GPNA) to glycylglycine, releasing yellow 5-amino-2-nitrobenzoate (or p-nitroaniline). The rate of yellow product formation is continuously monitored at 405–410 nm and is directly proportional to GGT activity.

format_list_numbered Usage Procedure

Continuous Kinetic GGT Activity Protocol

1

Reagent Pre-warming

Pre-warm Buffer and Substrate reagents in a 37°C water bath or incubator for 15 minutes before assay.

schedule 15 mins
2

Sample & Working Solution Addition

Pipette 10 µL of serum, plasma, or clarified tissue homogenate into designated microplate wells. Immediately add 100 µL of working substrate mix (Buffer + Substrate 4:1 v/v).

schedule 2 mins
3

Kinetic Absorbance Measurement

Immediately place plate in a pre-warmed 37°C microplate reader. Read initial absorbance A1 at 405 nm (or 410 nm) at t = 1 min, and final absorbance A2 at t = 6 min (5-minute reaction interval). Calculate ΔA/min.

schedule 5 mins

p-Nitroaniline Standard Curve Protocol

1

Prepare p-Nitroaniline Standards

Prepare calibration standards: 0, 200, 400, 500, 600, 800, 900, 1000 µmol/L in buffer. Pipette 10 µL per well.

schedule 10 mins
2

Incubation & Readout

Add 100 µL buffer mix, incubate at 37°C for 5 minutes, and read absorbance at 405–410 nm. Plot linear standard curve ΔA = ax + b.

schedule 10 mins

calculate Kinetic Formula & Calculation (EEA029)

Kinetic Formula: GGT (U/L) = (ΔA/min × Vtotal × 10⁶) / (ε × d × Vsample) × f Tissue Formula: GGT (U/gprot) = GGT (U/L) / Cpr

1. Kinetic Calculation: $\Delta A/ ext{min} = rac{A_2 - A_1}{T}$ (where $T = 5 ext{ min}$). $\epsilon$ is the millimolar extinction coefficient of 5-amino-2-nitrobenzoate ($9.5 ext{ L}\cdot ext{mmol}^{-1}\cdot ext{cm}^{-1}$ at 405 nm); $d$ = light path length ($0.6 ext{ cm}$ in standard 96-well); $V_{total} = 110 ext{ µL}$; $V_{sample} = 10 ext{ µL}$.

2. Simplified Formula: $ ext{Serum GGT (U/L)} = \Delta A/ ext{min} imes 1158 imes f$.

3. Standard Curve Method: Plot standard OD as y-axis against concentration as x-axis. Fit linear curve: $\Delta A = ax + b$ with a = 0.001824, b = 0.01108.

Storage & Stability

Store kit at 2–8°C or -20°C according to component labels. Protect chromogenic substrate from light.

event_available Stable for 12 months when properly stored.

warning Cautions & Safety

  • check_circle Maintain microplate reader chamber at exactly 37°C during kinetic monitoring.
  • check_circle Avoid bubble formation during pipetting as bubbles will severely distort kinetic OD slopes.
  • check_circle Heparin and EDTA plasma are acceptable; do not use fluoride or citrate.
analytics

Expected Results

Normal adult human serum GGT ranges from 10–60 U/L. GGT is highly sensitive to alcohol-induced liver injury and biliary obstruction.
