CD68 (KP1) & Myeloperoxidase (MPO) IHC Staining Protocol

biotech BioCore Pathology / Multi-Vendor Standard • Dual-Marker Myeloid Infiltrate Panel (FFPE & Frozen)

info Overview & Principle

This standardized clinical pathology SOP describes operational, bench-level chromogenic (DAB) and immunofluorescence immunohistochemistry (IHC) protocols for two complementary markers widely used together to characterize tissue myeloid infiltrate in human and animal research specimens.

Marker Clone / Type Host / Isotype Lineage Identified Primary Reference
CD68 Monoclonal, clone KP1 Mouse IgG1, kappa Monocyte / macrophage lineage (cytoplasmic/lysosomal granules) Pulford et al., J Clin Pathol 1989;42:414–21
MPO Polyclonal Rabbit IgG Neutrophilic granulocyte lineage, all maturation stages (azurophilic granules) Agilent/Dako (Code A0398)

Separate single-marker protocols are provided for each antibody, split into an FFPE version (with heat-induced epitope retrieval and 3% H₂O₂ quench) and a Frozen-Section version (acetone fixation, 0.3% H₂O₂ quench to protect uncrosslinked morphology, and detergent-free workflow to preserve granular MPO retention).

format_list_numbered Usage Procedure

Protocol A — CD68 Monoclonal Antibody (Clone KP1), FFPE Sections

1

Sectioning & Slide Baking

Cut FFPE block at 4 µm thickness; float section on a 45–50°C water bath; mount onto a positively charged slide (e.g. SuperFrost Plus). Air-dry vertically 10–15 min, then bake horizontally at 56–60°C for 60 min to ensure adhesion.

schedule 75 mins
2

Deparaffinization

Immerse slides in Xylene jar 1 for 5 min -> transfer to fresh Xylene jar 2 for 5 min.

schedule 10 mins
3

Graded Ethanol Rehydration

100% ethanol (2 changes × 5 min) -> 95% ethanol (5 min) -> 70% ethanol (5 min) -> rinse in running distilled water for 2 min. From this point, do not let sections dry out at any step.

infoKeep slides immersed in wash buffer or distilled water between steps.
schedule 22 mins
4

Heat-Induced Epitope Retrieval (HIER)

Transfer slides into pre-warmed 10 mM citrate buffer, pH 6.0. Heat in a steamer / water bath at 95–99°C for 20 min (alternative: pressure cooker at 121°C for 3 min, if locally validated). Turn off heat; cool slides inside the buffer at room temperature for 20–30 min before removing.

infoRapid cooling or buffer-swapping while hot risks tissue detachment.
schedule 45 mins
5

Wash & Hydrophobic Barrier

Wash in TBST (2 × 5 min) with gentle agitation. Carefully wipe excess buffer around the tissue and circle each section with a PAP pen ~2–3 mm outside the tissue edge; let dry 1–2 min.

schedule 12 mins
6

Endogenous Peroxidase Quench

Apply ~100–150 µL of freshly diluted 3% H₂O₂ (1:10 in methanol or distilled water) to fully cover the section. Incubate 10 min at room temperature in a closed slide box (protect from strong light). Wash in TBST (2 × 5 min).

schedule 20 mins
7

Protein Serum Blocking

Apply ~100–150 µL of 10% normal goat serum (matched to secondary host) + 1% BSA in TBST. Incubate 20–30 min at room temperature in a humidified chamber. Gently tip off excess without washing, then proceed directly to primary antibody.

schedule 30 mins
8

Primary Antibody Incubation (CD68 KP1)

Dilute CD68 (KP1) to 1:50–1:100 in antibody diluent (1% BSA + 0.05% Tween-20 in TBS). Apply ~100 µL per section. Incubate 30–60 min at room temperature, or 4°C overnight in a lidded humidified chamber. In parallel, process one mouse IgG1 isotype-control slide and one no-primary control slide identically.

schedule 30–60 min (or O/N)
9

Wash & Polymer Secondary Detection

Wash in TBST (3 × 5 min) with complete buffer exchange each time. Apply ~100–150 µL of ready-to-use HRP-polymer anti-mouse reagent. Incubate 30 min at room temperature. Wash in TBST (3 × 5 min).

schedule 45 mins
10

DAB Chromogen Substrate Reaction

Prepare DAB working solution immediately before use (1 drop / 20 µL DAB concentrate per 1.0 mL substrate buffer). Apply ~100 µL per section. Develop 5–10 min at room temperature, checking color under microscope every 2–3 min; stop by immersion in distilled water once brown granular signal is adequate relative to background.

schedule 5–10 mins
11

Counterstain, Bluing, Dehydration & Mounting

Rinse in running distilled water (5 min). Counterstain with Mayer's hematoxylin (1–2 min); rinse tap water (1–2 min). Blue in ammonia water (0.1–1%) or Scott's substitute for 30–60 sec until nuclei turn blue; rinse tap water (1–2 min). Dehydrate: 70% ethanol 1 min -> 95% ethanol 1 min -> 100% ethanol (2 × 2 min). Clear in xylene (2 × 2–5 min). Mount with permanent resinous medium (DPX) and coverslip.

schedule 25 mins

Protocol B — CD68 Monoclonal Antibody (Clone KP1), Frozen Sections

1

Cryosectioning & Storage

Cut fresh-frozen OCT-embedded tissue at 5–8 µm on a cryostat (chamber –18 to –22°C / specimen head –18 to –20°C); thaw-mount onto charged slides. Air-dry at room temperature for 30–60 min. (If stored at -80°C, thaw inside original sealed wrapping at room temp for 20–30 min to prevent condensation directly on tissue).

schedule 45 mins
2

Cold Acetone Fixation

Immerse in pre-cooled (–20°C) 100% acetone for 10 min. Remove and air-dry at room temperature for 5–10 min before any aqueous step (do NOT rinse immediately; let acetone evaporate fully). Circle section with a PAP pen.

schedule 20 mins
3

Wash & Peroxidase Quench (0.3% H₂O₂)

Wash in PBS or TBS (2 × 5 min). Apply ~100–150 µL of freshly diluted 0.3% H₂O₂ (diluted 1:100 in methanol or PBS) for 10 min at room temperature. Wash in PBS/TBS (2 × 5 min).

info0.3% H₂O₂ (rather than 3%) is used for uncrosslinked frozen tissue to prevent oxidative damage and epitope loss.
schedule 20 mins
4

Protein Block & Primary Antibody

Block with ~100–150 µL of 10% normal serum + 1% BSA for 20–30 min. Apply CD68 (KP1) diluted 1:50 in antibody diluent (~100 µL). Incubate 30–60 min at room temperature, or 4°C overnight in a humidified chamber. Run isotype-control and no-primary slides in parallel. Wash in PBS/TBS (3 × 5 min).

schedule 30–60 min (or O/N)
5

Chromogenic Detection (DAB Branch)

Apply ~100–150 µL ready-to-use HRP-polymer anti-mouse reagent for 30 min at room temperature. Wash (3 × 5 min). Apply freshly prepared DAB working solution for 5–10 min (monitor under microscope). Rinse, counterstain with Mayer's hematoxylin, blue, dehydrate, clear, and mount with permanent medium.

schedule 45 mins
6

Alternative: Immunofluorescence Branch (IF)

Omit DAB steps. Apply fluorophore-conjugated anti-mouse secondary (1:200–1:500) for 30–60 min at room temperature in the dark. Wash in PBS (3 × 5 min). Counterstain with DAPI (300 nM, 5 min). Mount with aqueous anti-fade mounting medium, seal edges, and store flat at 4°C protected from light.

schedule 60 mins

Protocol C — Myeloperoxidase (MPO) Polyclonal Antibody, FFPE Sections

1

Sectioning, Deparaffinization & Rehydration

Cut FFPE block at 4 µm; float on 45–50°C water bath; mount on charged slides. Bake horizontally at 56–60°C for 60 min. Deparaffinize in Xylene (2 changes × 5 min). Rehydrate through 100% ethanol (2 × 5 min) -> 95% ethanol (5 min) -> 70% ethanol (5 min) -> rinse in running distilled water for 2 min.

schedule 90 mins
2

Antigen Retrieval (HIER)

Immerse slides in 10 mM citrate buffer pH 6.0 (or Tris-EDTA buffer pH 9.0). Heat at 95–99°C for 20 min; cool in buffer at room temperature for 20–30 min. Wash in TBST (2 × 5 min). Circle section with PAP pen and let dry.

schedule 50 mins
3

Peroxidase Quench & Protein Serum Block

Apply ~100–150 µL of freshly diluted 3% H₂O₂ for 10 min at room temperature (essential to quench endogenous neutrophil peroxidase). Wash TBST (2 × 5 min). Apply 10% normal goat serum + 1% BSA in TBST for 20–30 min. Tip off excess without washing.

schedule 40 mins
4

MPO Primary Antibody Incubation

Dilute MPO polyclonal antibody to 1:300–1:600 in antibody diluent (titrate against positive control tissue). Apply ~100 µL per section. Incubate 30–60 min at room temperature, or 4°C overnight in a humidified chamber. In parallel, run non-immune rabbit serum/IgG and no-primary controls. Wash in TBST (3 × 5 min).

schedule 30–60 min (or O/N)
5

HRP-Polymer Anti-Rabbit & DAB Chromogen

Apply ~100–150 µL ready-to-use HRP-polymer anti-rabbit secondary reagent for 30 min at room temperature. Wash in TBST (3 × 5 min). Apply freshly prepared DAB working solution for 5–10 min, monitoring under microscope until brown cytoplasmic granules in neutrophils are distinct. Stop in distilled water.

schedule 45 mins
6

Counterstain, Dehydration & Mounting

Counterstain in Mayer's hematoxylin (1–2 min), rinse in tap water, blue in ammonia water (30–60 sec), rinse. Dehydrate through graded ethanol (70%, 95%, 100% × 2), clear in xylene (2 × 2–5 min), and mount with permanent resinous medium.

schedule 25 mins

Protocol D — Myeloperoxidase (MPO) Polyclonal Antibody, Frozen Sections

1

Cryosectioning & Acetone Fixation

Cut fresh-frozen OCT tissue at 5–8 µm; thaw-mount on charged slides; air-dry 30–60 min at room temperature. Fix in pre-cooled (–20°C) 100% acetone for 10 min. Air-dry at room temperature 5–10 min. Circle with PAP pen. Wash in PBS/TBS (2 × 5 min).

schedule 60 mins
2

Gentle Peroxidase Quench (0.3% H₂O₂)

Apply ~100–150 µL of freshly diluted 0.3% H₂O₂ in methanol or PBS for 10 min at room temperature. (Quenches endogenous neutrophil peroxidase without destroying uncrosslinked frozen tissue architecture). Wash in PBS/TBS (2 × 5 min).

infoCRITICAL: Do NOT use detergent permeabilization; MPO is soluble and detergent causes granule content diffusion.
schedule 20 mins
3

Protein Block & MPO Primary Incubation

Apply 10% normal goat serum + 1% BSA for 20–30 min. Apply MPO polyclonal antibody diluted 1:150–1:300 in antibody diluent (~100 µL). Incubate 30–60 min at room temperature, or 4°C overnight in a humidified chamber. Wash PBS/TBS (3 × 5 min).

schedule 30–60 min (or O/N)
4

Chromogenic Detection (DAB Branch)

Apply ready-to-use HRP-polymer anti-rabbit secondary for 30 min. Wash (3 × 5 min). Develop in DAB for 5–10 min (monitor under microscope). Rinse, counterstain with Mayer's hematoxylin, blue, dehydrate, clear, and mount with permanent resinous medium.

schedule 45 mins
5

Alternative: Immunofluorescence Branch (IF)

Omit DAB. Apply fluorophore-conjugated anti-rabbit secondary (1:200–1:500) for 30–60 min in the dark. Wash PBS (3 × 5 min). Counterstain with DAPI (300 nM, 5 min). Mount with aqueous anti-fade mounting medium.

schedule 60 mins

Master Buffer Recipes & Multi-Level Run Controls

1

Master Buffer Recipes Matrix

• Citrate Buffer (10 mM, pH 6.0): 2.94 g tri-sodium citrate dihydrate in 1 L ddH2O + 0.5 mL Tween-20 (0.05%), adjust pH to 6.0 with 1 N HCl. • Tris-EDTA Buffer (10 mM Tris / 1 mM EDTA, pH 9.0): 1.21 g Tris base + 0.37 g disodium EDTA in 1 L ddH2O + 0.5 mL Tween-20 (0.05%), adjust pH to 9.0. • TBST Wash Buffer (1X): 0.05 M Tris-HCl, 0.15 M NaCl, pH 7.6 + 0.05% Tween-20. • PBS (1X): 137 mM NaCl, 2.7 mM KCl, 10 mM Na2HPO4, 1.8 mM KH2PO4, pH 7.4. • H2O2 Working Solution: FFPE = 3% H2O2 (1:10 dilution of 30% stock in methanol/PBS); Frozen = 0.3% H2O2 (1:100 dilution). • Protein/Serum Block: 10% normal goat serum + 1% BSA in TBST or PBS. • DAB Working Solution: 1 drop (~20 µL) chromogen concentrate per 1.0 mL substrate buffer (mix immediately before use; protect from light).

schedule Recipes
2

Multi-Level Quality Controls Protocol

• Positive Tissue Controls: Human tonsil or lymph node for CD68 (germinal center macrophages, sinus histiocytes); Human tonsil, spleen, or bone marrow for MPO (granulocytes, intensely positive in splenic red pulp). • Negative Reagent Controls: Replace primary antibody with mouse IgG1 isotype control (for CD68) or non-immune rabbit serum/IgG (for MPO) at matched final concentration on serial sections. • Negative Tissue Controls: Human colon epithelium serves as built-in negative tissue control for MPO (epithelium is completely negative; scattered stromal granulocytes serve as internal positive check).

Troubleshooting Matrix & Evidence-Based Technical Notes

1

Troubleshooting Guide

• High Background: Insufficient blocking, primary too concentrated, or inadequate washing. -> Remedy: Extend serum block (30–60 min); dilute primary further; increase wash volume and complete buffer exchanges. • Weak or Absent Signal: Under-retrieval, antibody too dilute, degraded reagent, or fixation > 48h. -> Remedy: Extend HIER time or switch buffer (citrate <-> Tris-EDTA); increase antibody concentration; verify reagent expiry. • Nonspecific Stromal / Fibroblast Staining with CD68: Known cross-reactivity of KP1 with a shared epitope outside macrophage lineage. -> Remedy: Confirm with cellular morphology and consider a secondary macrophage marker (e.g. CD163). • Weak MPO in Confirmed Neutrophils: Insufficient retrieval or low antibody titer for that lot. -> Remedy: Re-titrate against positive control (tonsil/spleen) for current lot.

schedule Troubleshooting
2

Technical Notes: Peroxidase Quench & Permeabilization

• Peroxidase Quench Concentration: FFPE uses 3% H2O2; Frozen sections use 0.3% H2O2. Acetone-fixed frozen tissue lacks formalin crosslinking and is susceptible to oxidative morphological damage and epitope loss at 3%. • On Permeabilization (Omitted for Frozen MPO): Acetone dissolves membrane lipids during fixation. Adding detergent (Triton X-100) to acetone-fixed MPO sections causes soluble MPO to leak from azurophilic granules, blurring discrete granular staining into diffuse background artifact.

Storage & Stability

Store primary antibodies at 4°C (CD68) and -20°C (MPO); Reagents at 15–25°C or 2–8°C.

event_available Buffers prepared fresh; FFPE slides baked 60 min at 60°C; frozen sections stored at -80°C.

warning Cautions & Safety

  • check_circle Always use 0.3% H₂O₂ (not 3%) for frozen sections to protect uncrosslinked tissue morphology and prevent epitope loss.
  • check_circle Do NOT add detergent permeabilization (e.g. Triton X-100) to acetone-fixed frozen sections for MPO; MPO is soluble and detergent causes artifactual leakage from azurophilic granules.
  • check_circle KP1 clone exhibits known cross-reactivity with a shared epitope on fibroblasts; interpret alongside cellular morphology and consider CD163 for confirmation.
  • check_circle DAB is a suspected carcinogen; handle in a chemical fume hood and neutralize with 10% sodium hypochlorite bleach before disposal.
analytics

Expected Results

Crisp granular cytoplasmic staining in tissue macrophages and Kupffer cells for CD68 (KP1), and intense azurophilic granular staining in polymorphonuclear neutrophils across all maturation stages for MPO, with clean background in non-myeloid parenchymal cells.
