# Immunohistochemical Staining Protocol: CD68 (Clone KP1) and Myeloperoxidase (Polyclonal)
## FFPE and Frozen Section Procedures — Bilingual Reference (EN / 中文)

# PART I — ENGLISH

## 1. Scope

This document describes chromogenic (DAB) immunohistochemistry (IHC) protocols for two markers commonly used together to characterize myeloid infiltrate in human tissue:

| Marker | Clone/Type | Host / Isotype | Lineage identified | Primary reference |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CD68 | Monoclonal, clone KP1 | Mouse IgG1, kappa | Monocyte/macrophage lineage (cytoplasmic/lysosomal granules) | Pulford et al., *J Clin Pathol* 1989;42(4):414–21 |
| MPO | Polyclonal | Rabbit IgG | Neutrophilic granulocyte lineage, all maturation stages (azurophilic granules) | Manufacturer package insert (e.g., Agilent/Dako A0398) |

Separate single-marker protocols are given for each antibody, each split into an FFPE version and a frozen-section version. Both antibodies are validated by manufacturers for both applications, but the quantitative parameters (dilution, retrieval time) published in package inserts are established primarily on FFPE tonsil; frozen-section parameters are comparatively under-documented in the primary literature and should be treated as a starting point for local titration (see Section 9).

Section 3–6 below give a fully operational, bench-level version of each protocol: exact reagent, concentration, applied volume, order of addition, and incubation condition for every step. Section 2.12 lists the buffer/reagent recipes referenced throughout.

## 2. Master Reagent and Materials List

### 2.1 Tissue handling / fixation
- 10% neutral buffered formalin (NBF) — FFPE fixation, 24–48 h
- OCT compound — frozen tissue embedding
- Cold acetone (–20 °C), or acetone:ethanol (1:1) — frozen section fixation
- Isopentane / liquid nitrogen — snap-freezing (if preparing fresh frozen blocks)

### 2.2 Sectioning
- Microtome + water bath (45–50 °C) — FFPE, 4 µm sections
- Cryostat (chamber –18 to –22 °C, specimen head –18 to –20 °C) — frozen, 5–8 µm sections
- Charged/adhesive slides (e.g., SuperFrost Plus)
- Hydrophobic barrier (PAP) pen

### 2.3 Deparaffinization / rehydration (FFPE only)
- Xylene (or xylene substitute, e.g., Histo-Clear) — 3 Coplin jars
- Graded ethanol: 100%, 95%, 70% — separate Coplin jars
- Deionized/distilled water

### 2.4 Antigen retrieval (FFPE only)
- Citrate buffer, 10 mM, pH 6.0 (recipe in 2.12)
- Tris-EDTA buffer, 10 mM Tris / 1 mM EDTA, pH 9.0 (recipe in 2.12)
- Heat source: pressure cooker, steamer, water bath, microwave, or automated retrieval station (e.g., PT Link)
- Plastic Coplin jar or slide-retrieval tray rated for the heating method used

### 2.5 Blocking
- 3% hydrogen peroxide, freshly prepared (recipe in 2.12) — endogenous peroxidase block
- 10% normal serum, species-matched to secondary, + 1% BSA (recipe in 2.12) — protein block
- Avidin/biotin blocking kit — only if using a biotin-based (ABC/LSAB) detection system

### 2.6 Primary antibodies
- CD68 Monoclonal Antibody, clone KP1 (mouse IgG1)
- MPO Polyclonal Antibody (rabbit IgG)
- Antibody diluent (commercial, or 1% BSA + 0.05% Tween-20 in TBS/PBS)

### 2.7 Negative-control reagents
- Mouse IgG1 isotype control, matched final concentration to CD68 primary
- Non-immune rabbit serum or rabbit IgG, matched final concentration to MPO primary
- Antibody diluent alone (no-primary control)

### 2.8 Detection system
- Polymer-based HRP anti-mouse detection kit (for CD68) — e.g., EnVision+/HRP or equivalent, ready-to-use
- Polymer-based HRP anti-rabbit detection kit (for MPO) — e.g., EnVision+/HRP or equivalent, ready-to-use
- (Alternative) Biotinylated secondary antibody + streptavidin-HRP (LSAB/ABC system)

### 2.9 Chromogen / counterstain / mounting
- DAB (3,3′-diaminobenzidine) chromogen-substrate kit (chromogen concentrate + substrate buffer, mixed immediately before use)
- Mayer's or Harris hematoxylin, ready-to-use — counterstain
- 1% acid alcohol (1% HCl in 70% ethanol) — differentiation, if needed
- Ammonia water (0.1–1%) or Scott's tap water substitute — bluing reagent
- Graded ethanol (70%, 95%, 100%) — dehydration
- Xylene — clearing
- Permanent mounting medium (e.g., DPX, Cytoseal) and coverslips

### 2.10 Buffers / general
- TBST or PBST wash buffer, 0.05% Tween-20 (recipe in 2.12)
- Humidified chamber (staining tray with damp paper towel/gauze at the base, lidded)
- pH meter, timer, Coplin jars/staining racks, transfer pipettes

### 2.11 Controls (tissue)
- Positive control (CD68): human tonsil or lymph node (macrophages in germinal centers, sinus histiocytes)
- Positive control (MPO): human tonsil, spleen, or bone marrow (granulocytes, strongly positive in splenic red pulp)
- Negative tissue control (MPO): colon (epithelium should be negative; scattered stromal/vascular granulocytes may still stain — this is expected)

### 2.12 Buffer and Reagent Preparation Recipes

| Reagent | Recipe |
|---|---|
| Citrate buffer, 10 mM, pH 6.0 (1×) | 2.94 g tri-sodium citrate dihydrate in 1 L distilled water; add 0.5 mL Tween-20 (final 0.05%); adjust to pH 6.0 with 1 N HCl. Prewarm to retrieval temperature before use. |
| Tris-EDTA buffer, pH 9.0 (1×) | 1.21 g Tris base + 0.37 g EDTA (disodium) in 1 L distilled water; add 0.5 mL Tween-20 (final 0.05%); adjust to pH 9.0 with NaOH/HCl. |
| TBST wash buffer (1×) | 0.05 M Tris-HCl, 0.15 M NaCl, pH 7.6, + 0.05% Tween-20. (Prepare as 10× Tris-buffered saline stock and dilute 1:10, then add Tween-20.) |
| PBS (1×) | 137 mM NaCl, 2.7 mM KCl, 10 mM Na₂HPO₄, 1.8 mM KH₂PO₄, pH 7.4. |
| H₂O₂ working solution (peroxidase block) | **FFPE:** 3% H₂O₂ — dilute 30% stock 1:10 in methanol (or in distilled water/PBS for an aqueous version). **Frozen sections: 0.3% H₂O₂** — dilute 30% stock 1:100 in methanol or PBS. The lower concentration is preferred for frozen/less-crosslinked tissue and for oxidation-sensitive epitopes (e.g., some cell-surface markers), since 3% H₂O₂ can cause morphological damage and epitope loss in the absence of formalin crosslinking; methanol also accelerates heme-group destruction, allowing effective endogenous-peroxidase quenching at the lower concentration and shorter or equal incubation time. Prepare immediately before use; discard after each session. |
| Protein/serum block | 10% normal serum (goat or horse — match to secondary antibody host species) + 1% BSA in TBST or PBS. |
| Antibody diluent (if not using commercial diluent) | 1% BSA + 0.05% Tween-20 in TBS or PBS. |
| DAB working solution | Mix chromogen concentrate into substrate buffer per kit instructions immediately before use (a common ratio is 1 drop, ≈20 µL, concentrate per 1 mL substrate buffer — confirm against your specific kit's insert). Mix by gentle inversion, not vortexing. Light-sensitive and unstable once mixed; prepare only the volume needed for the current run. |
| Bluing reagent | 0.1–1% ammonia water in tap water, or a commercial Scott's tap water substitute (sodium bicarbonate/magnesium sulfate solution). |

Reagent volumes below assume a standard tissue section of roughly 1 cm² framed by a hydrophobic barrier pen; scale proportionally for larger sections.

## 3. Protocol A — CD68 (KP1), FFPE Sections

**Bench setup:** 3 Coplin jars of xylene, 4 jars of graded ethanol (100% ×2, 95%, 70%), running distilled water, pre-warmed citrate buffer pH 6.0 in a retrieval-safe vessel, TBST wash buffer, freshly diluted 3% H₂O₂, protein block, primary antibody diluted to working concentration, ready-to-use HRP-polymer anti-mouse secondary, freshly mixed DAB working solution, Mayer's hematoxylin, ammonia water, dehydration/clearing series, mounting medium, PAP pen, humidified chamber, timer.

1. **Sectioning:** Cut FFPE block at 4 µm; float section on a 45–50 °C water bath; mount onto a positively charged slide. Air-dry vertically 10–15 min, then bake horizontally at 56–60 °C for 60 min to ensure adhesion.
2. **Deparaffinization:** Xylene jar 1, 5 min → xylene jar 2 (fresh), 5 min.
3. **Rehydration:** 100% ethanol, 2 changes × 5 min → 95% ethanol, 5 min → 70% ethanol, 5 min → rinse in running distilled water, 2 min. From this point, do not let sections dry out at any step.
4. **Antigen retrieval:** Transfer slides into pre-warmed 10 mM citrate buffer, pH 6.0. Heat in a steamer/water bath at 95–99 °C for 20 min (alternative: pressure cooker at 121 °C, ~3 min, only if this exact regimen has been separately validated in your lab). Turn off heat; cool slides inside the buffer at room temperature for 20–30 min before removing (rapid cooling or buffer-swapping while hot risks tissue detachment).
5. **Wash:** TBST, 2 × 5 min, gentle agitation.
6. **Barrier:** Circle each section with a PAP pen ~2–3 mm outside the tissue edge; let dry 1–2 min. This confines the ~100–150 µL reagent volumes used in subsequent steps to the tissue area.
7. **Peroxidase block:** Apply ~100–150 µL of freshly diluted 3% H₂O₂ to fully cover the section. Incubate 10 min, room temperature, in a closed slide box (protect from strong light). Wash TBST, 2 × 5 min.
8. **Protein block:** Apply ~100–150 µL of 10% normal serum (matched to secondary host, e.g., normal goat serum) + 1% BSA in TBST. Incubate 20–30 min, room temperature, humidified chamber. Gently tip off excess without washing, then proceed directly to the primary antibody.
9. **Primary antibody:** Dilute CD68 (KP1) to 1:50–1:100 in antibody diluent (starting range; confirm by local titration). Apply ~100 µL per section, ensuring full coverage. Incubate 30–60 min at room temperature, or 4 °C overnight in a lidded humidified chamber. In parallel, process one isotype-control slide (mouse IgG1 at the same final concentration) and one no-primary slide (diluent only) identically.
10. **Wash:** TBST, 3 × 5 min, complete buffer exchange each time.
11. **Secondary/detection:** Apply ~100–150 µL of ready-to-use HRP-polymer anti-mouse reagent. Incubate 30 min, room temperature. Wash TBST, 3 × 5 min.
12. **Chromogen:** Prepare DAB working solution immediately before use (see 2.12). Apply ~100 µL per section. Develop 5–10 min at room temperature, checking color under the microscope every 2–3 min; stop by immersion in distilled water once brown signal is adequate relative to background (over-development raises background).
13. **Rinse:** Running distilled water, 5 min.
14. **Counterstain:** Mayer's hematoxylin, 1–2 min. Rinse under running tap water until it runs clear (1–2 min).
15. **Bluing:** Ammonia water (or Scott's substitute), 30–60 sec until nuclei turn blue. Rinse under running tap water, 1–2 min.
16. **Dehydration:** 70% ethanol 1 min → 95% ethanol 1 min → 100% ethanol, 2 × 2 min.
17. **Clearing:** Xylene, 2 × 2–5 min.
18. **Mounting:** Apply 1–2 drops of permanent mounting medium; lower coverslip at a shallow angle to avoid trapping air bubbles. Cure flat, protected from dust, for several hours (overnight for full cure) before imaging.

**Expected pattern:** granular cytoplasmic staining in macrophages, histiocytes, and Kupffer-cell–type cells. Note the known cross-reactivity of KP1 toward fibroblasts (shared epitope, not true CD68 expression) — interpret alongside morphology.

## 4. Protocol B — CD68 (KP1), Frozen Sections

**Bench setup:** cryostat set to chamber –18 to –22 °C / specimen head –18 to –20 °C, pre-cooled 100% acetone (–20 °C), PBS or TBS wash buffer, freshly diluted 0.3% H₂O₂ (chromogenic workflow only), protein block, primary antibody, HRP-polymer anti-mouse secondary (or fluorophore-conjugated anti-mouse secondary for IF), DAB working solution or aqueous mounting medium, PAP pen, humidified chamber.

1. **Cryosectioning:** Cut fresh-frozen OCT-embedded tissue at 5–8 µm; thaw-mount onto a charged slide (touch a room-temperature slide to the section to melt it onto the glass). Air-dry at room temperature 30–60 min. If not staining immediately, wrap individually and store at –80 °C (stable for several months); before use, thaw within the original wrapping at room temperature for 20–30 min to avoid condensation directly on the tissue.
2. **Fixation:** Immerse in pre-cooled (–20 °C) 100% acetone, 10 min. Remove and air-dry at room temperature 5–10 min before any aqueous step (do not rinse immediately after acetone; let it evaporate fully).
3. **Barrier:** Circle each section with a PAP pen once dry.
4. **Wash:** PBS or TBS, 2 × 5 min, gentle agitation.
5. **Peroxidase block (chromogenic workflow only — omit for immunofluorescence):** ~100–150 µL of 0.3% H₂O₂ (diluted in methanol or PBS — see 2.12), 10 min, room temperature. The lower concentration, rather than the 3% used for FFPE, is used here because acetone-fixed frozen tissue lacks formalin crosslinking and is more susceptible to oxidative morphological damage and epitope loss at higher peroxide concentrations. Wash PBS/TBS, 2 × 5 min.
6. **Protein block:** ~100–150 µL of 10% normal serum (matched to secondary host) + 1% BSA, 20–30 min, room temperature, humidified chamber.
7. **Primary antibody:** CD68 (KP1) diluted to 1:50 as a starting point (titrate locally — frozen-section optimal dilution is less standardized than the FFPE range). Apply ~100 µL per section. Incubate 30–60 min room temperature, or 4 °C overnight, humidified chamber. Run isotype-control and no-primary slides in parallel.
8. **Wash:** PBS/TBS, 3 × 5 min.
9. **Secondary — chromogenic branch:** ~100–150 µL ready-to-use HRP-polymer anti-mouse reagent, 30 min room temperature. Wash 3 × 5 min.
10. **Chromogen — chromogenic branch:** Prepare and apply DAB as in Protocol A step 12; develop 5–10 min, monitor under microscope; rinse.
11. **Counterstain/dehydrate/clear/mount — chromogenic branch:** identical to Protocol A steps 14–18.

**Immunofluorescence branch (replace steps 9–11 if IF is preferred over DAB):**
9a. Apply fluorophore-conjugated anti-mouse secondary, typically 1:200–1:500 in antibody diluent, ~100 µL per section. Incubate 30–60 min, room temperature, protected from light.
10a. Wash PBS, 3 × 5 min, in the dark.
11a. Optional nuclear counterstain: DAPI, 300 nM, 5 min, room temperature, dark; rinse briefly.
12a. Mount with an aqueous anti-fade mounting medium; seal coverslip edges with clear nail polish if not imaging same-day; store flat at 4 °C, protected from light.

## 5. Protocol C — MPO Polyclonal, FFPE Sections

**Bench setup:** as Protocol A, substituting an HRP-polymer anti-rabbit secondary and non-immune rabbit serum/IgG for the negative control.

1–3. **Sectioning, deparaffinization, rehydration:** identical to Protocol A, steps 1–3.
4. **Antigen retrieval:** Citrate buffer pH 6.0 or Tris-EDTA buffer pH 9.0 (both cited as effective for this target in manufacturer documentation) — 95–99 °C, 20 min; cool 20–30 min in buffer. (Validated alternative regimen: pH 7.8 retrieval buffer, autoclave, 121 °C, 5 min — use only if this specific combination has been validated in your setting.)
5. **Wash:** TBST, 2 × 5 min.
6. **Barrier:** PAP pen circle, dry 1–2 min.
7. **Peroxidase block:** ~100–150 µL of 3% H₂O₂, 10 min, room temperature. Wash TBST, 2 × 5 min.
8. **Protein block:** ~100–150 µL of 10% normal serum (goat, matched to anti-rabbit secondary) + 1% BSA, 20–30 min, room temperature.
9. **Primary antibody:** MPO polyclonal diluted 1:300–1:600 (manufacturer FFPE starting range for this reagent; some lots/vendors require concentrations as strong as 1:150 — titrate against your specific lot on the positive control tissue before running experimental sections). Apply ~100 µL per section. Incubate 30–60 min room temperature, or 4 °C overnight. Run a non-immune rabbit serum/IgG control and a no-primary control in parallel, matched to the same final concentration where applicable.
10. **Wash:** TBST, 3 × 5 min.
11. **Secondary:** ~100–150 µL ready-to-use HRP-polymer anti-rabbit reagent, 30 min, room temperature. Wash TBST, 3 × 5 min.
12. **Chromogen:** Prepare and apply DAB as in Protocol A step 12; develop 5–10 min, monitor under microscope.
13–18. **Rinse, counterstain, blue, dehydrate, clear, mount:** identical to Protocol A steps 13–18.

**Expected pattern:** strong, diffuse cytoplasmic/granular staining in neutrophilic granulocytes at all maturation stages; weak/variable in monocyte-lineage cells; negative in lymphoid, erythroid, megakaryocytic, stromal dendritic, mast, and endothelial cells. Use as part of a panel, not in isolation, for lineage discrimination.

## 6. Protocol D — MPO Polyclonal, Frozen Sections

1–4. **Cryosectioning, fixation, barrier, wash:** identical to Protocol B, steps 1–4.
5. **Peroxidase block (chromogenic workflow only):** ~100–150 µL of 0.3% H₂O₂ (diluted in methanol or PBS — see 2.12), 10 min, room temperature. As in Protocol B, the lower concentration protects acetone-fixed tissue from oxidative damage; it is arguably even more relevant here, since MPO is itself a heme-containing peroxidase, so this step is quenching the activity of the very enzyme class being targeted, in the tissue's endogenous granulocytes, not just an unrelated background enzyme. Wash PBS/TBS, 2 × 5 min.
6. **Protein block:** ~100–150 µL of 10% normal serum (goat, matched to anti-rabbit secondary) + 1% BSA, 20–30 min.
7. **Primary antibody:** MPO polyclonal, starting dilution 1:150–1:300 (more concentrated than the FFPE range, since HIER-mediated epitope enhancement is absent) — titrate locally. Apply ~100 µL per section. Incubate 30–60 min room temperature, or 4 °C overnight. Non-immune rabbit serum/IgG and no-primary controls run in parallel.
8. **Wash:** PBS/TBS, 3 × 5 min.
9. **Secondary — chromogenic branch:** ~100–150 µL ready-to-use HRP-polymer anti-rabbit reagent, 30 min. Wash 3 × 5 min.
10. **Chromogen — chromogenic branch:** DAB, 5–10 min, monitored under microscope; rinse.
11. **Counterstain/dehydrate/clear/mount:** identical to Protocol A steps 14–18.

**Immunofluorescence branch:** replace steps 9–11 with a fluorophore-conjugated anti-rabbit secondary (~1:200–1:500, 30–60 min, dark), optional DAPI (300 nM, 5 min), and aqueous anti-fade mounting — parallel to Protocol B's IF branch.

## 7. Run Controls (apply to all four protocols)

- **Positive tissue control:** processed in parallel with test sections, same fixation/retrieval, every staining run.
- **Negative reagent control:** primary antibody replaced with diluent alone, or with an isotype/non-immune control at matched concentration, on a serial or adjacent section.
- **Internal negative control:** cell types within the test section known not to express the target (e.g., colon epithelium relative to MPO) provide an additional built-in check.
- Document antibody lot number, retrieval conditions, and incubation times for each run; re-titrate when lot changes are noted for the polyclonal MPO reagent in particular, since polyclonal lot-to-lot titer variability is greater than for the monoclonal CD68 reagent.

## 8. Troubleshooting (brief)

| Problem | Likely cause | Adjustment |
|---|---|---|
| High background | Insufficient blocking; primary too concentrated; inadequate washing | Extend serum block; dilute primary further; increase wash volume/duration |
| Weak or absent signal | Under-retrieval; antibody too dilute; degraded reagent; fixation over 48 h | Extend HIER time or switch buffer (citrate ↔ Tris-EDTA); increase antibody concentration; check reagent expiry |
| Nonspecific stromal/fibroblast staining with CD68 | Known cross-reactivity of KP1 with a shared epitope outside the macrophage lineage | Confirm with morphology and, if needed, a second macrophage marker (e.g., CD163) |
| Weak MPO in confirmed neutrophils | Insufficient retrieval or antibody titer too low for that lot | Re-titrate against positive control (tonsil/spleen) for the current lot |

## 9. Notes on Evidence and Parameter Certainty

**Established, manufacturer- and literature-documented:**
- CD68 (KP1) targets a lysosomal/cytoplasmic glycoprotein and marks monocyte/macrophage-lineage cells, with documented, non-lineage-specific reactivity toward fibroblasts via a shared epitope.
- MPO polyclonal antibody marks myeloid (granulocytic) lineage cells at all maturation stages, is weak/variable in monocytes, and is negative in lymphoid, erythroid, megakaryocytic, and stromal/vascular cells.
- Both antibodies are manufacturer-validated for FFPE and frozen-section IHC applications.
- The general IHC step sequence and standard buffer recipes given in 2.12 are established laboratory methodology, independent of the specific antibodies used.

**Vendor/lot-dependent, requiring local validation before diagnostic or reportable use:**
- Exact working dilution: published values range from roughly 1:50 to 1:100 (original Dako clone, FFPE) up to 1:3000 for some recombinant/higher-titer CD68 (KP1) preparations from other suppliers; MPO polyclonal working dilutions in the literature range from about 1:150 to 1:600 depending on supplier and lot.
- Exact retrieval buffer and duration: both citrate pH 6.0 and Tris-EDTA pH 9.0 are cited as effective for these targets, but relative performance can differ by fixation duration and detection-system sensitivity.
- Frozen-section-specific dilution and incubation parameters are comparatively less documented in manufacturer package inserts (built primarily around FFPE tonsil validation) than FFPE parameters; the frozen-section dilutions above are reasonable starting points, not manufacturer-certified values.
- Applied reagent volumes (~100–150 µL) and blocking/incubation times above follow common bench convention rather than a single universal standard; adjust for section size and the specific detection kit's package insert where available.

Given this variability, the protocol above should be treated as a validated starting point. Consistent with standard laboratory-accreditation practice for IHC, each new antibody lot and each new tissue type should be titrated against a known positive control before results are used for interpretation or publication.

**Revision note (peroxidase block, frozen sections):** an earlier version of this document used 3% H₂O₂ uniformly across all four protocols. This has been corrected for the frozen-section protocols (B, D) to 0.3% H₂O₂, which is the concentration generally recommended for acetone-fixed frozen tissue in the literature, since such tissue lacks formalin crosslinking and is more prone to oxidative damage and epitope loss at 3%. The FFPE protocols (A, C) retain 3% H₂O₂, consistent with standard practice for formalin-crosslinked tissue.

**On permeabilization (not added to this protocol):** acetone fixation of frozen sections is a solvent-based fixation method that dissolves membrane lipids as part of the fixation process itself, and general methodology sources characterize acetone/alcohol-based fixation as not routinely requiring a separate detergent permeabilization step, in contrast to aldehyde-crosslinked (e.g., PFA-fixed) tissue. Published acetone-fixed protocols for granule/lysosome-associated antigens, including CD68 and MPO, generally proceed directly from fixation to blocking without an added detergent step. Adding a nonionic detergent (e.g., Triton X-100) is not clearly established as necessary here, and carries a real trade-off for this specific application: MPO is a soluble enzyme within azurophilic granules, and more aggressive membrane disruption risks allowing granule contents to diffuse before antibody binding, which would blur the discrete granular staining pattern that is used to distinguish genuine neutrophil-lineage signal from diffuse background — a pattern-recognition consideration, not just a sensitivity one. This document does not add a detergent step for that reason; it remains a reasonable option to test empirically if signal is unexpectedly weak after confirming retrieval/dilution are not the limiting factor.

本方案描述了两种常用于评估组织中髓系细胞浸润情况的标志物的显色（DAB）免疫组织化学（IHC）染色流程：

|---|---|---|---|---|
| CD68 | 单克隆抗体，克隆号 KP1 | 小鼠 IgG1, kappa | 单核/巨噬细胞谱系（胞浆/溶酶体颗粒） | Pulford 等，*J Clin Pathol* 1989;42(4):414–21 |
| MPO（髓过氧化物酶） | 多克隆抗体 | 兔 IgG | 中性粒细胞谱系，各分化成熟阶段（嗜天青颗粒） | 厂商说明书（如 Agilent/Dako A0398） |

以下分别给出两种抗体各自独立的单标染色流程，每种抗体再细分为石蜡切片（FFPE）版本和冰冻切片版本。第 3–6 节给出每一步具体到试剂、浓度、加样量、加入顺序及操作细节的可直接上手操作版本；第 2.12 节列出全文引用到的缓冲液/试剂配方。

## 2. 所需试剂与耗材总清单

### 2.1 组织处理/固定
- 10% 中性缓冲福尔马林（NBF）——石蜡切片固定，24–48 小时
- OCT 包埋剂——冰冻组织包埋
- 预冷丙酮（–20 °C），或丙酮:乙醇（1:1）——冰冻切片固定
- 异戊烷/液氮——用于新鲜组织速冻包埋（如需自行制作冰冻块）

### 2.2 切片
- 切片机 + 展片水浴箱（45–50 °C）——石蜡切片，厚度 4 μm
- 冷冻切片机（腔体温度 –18 至 –22 °C，样本头 –18 至 –20 °C）——冰冻切片，厚度 5–8 μm
- 荷电/黏附载玻片（如 SuperFrost Plus）
- 疏水屏障笔（PAP pen）

### 2.3 脱蜡/复水（仅石蜡切片需要）
- 二甲苯（或替代性脱蜡剂，如 Histo-Clear）——3 个染色缸
- 梯度乙醇：100%、95%、70%——分别独立染色缸
- 去离子水/蒸馏水

### 2.4 抗原修复（仅石蜡切片需要）
- 柠檬酸盐缓冲液，10 mM，pH 6.0（配方见 2.12）
- Tris-EDTA 缓冲液，10 mM Tris / 1 mM EDTA，pH 9.0（配方见 2.12）
- 加热设备：高压锅、蒸锅、水浴箱、微波炉，或自动化抗原修复仪（如 PT Link）
- 适配所选加热方式的塑料染色缸或修复托盘

### 2.5 封闭试剂
- 3% 过氧化氢，现配现用（配方见 2.12）——内源性过氧化物酶阻断
- 10% 与二抗种属匹配的正常血清 + 1% BSA（配方见 2.12）——蛋白封闭
- 亲和素/生物素封闭试剂盒——仅在使用生物素类检测系统（ABC/LSAB）时需要

### 2.6 一抗
- CD68 单克隆抗体，克隆号 KP1（小鼠 IgG1）
- MPO 多克隆抗体（兔 IgG）
- 抗体稀释液（商品化稀释液，或 1% BSA + 0.05% Tween-20 溶于 TBS/PBS）

### 2.7 阴性对照相关试剂
- 小鼠 IgG1 同型对照抗体，终浓度与 CD68 一抗一致
- 非免疫兔血清或兔 IgG，终浓度与 MPO 一抗一致
- 单独使用抗体稀释液（不加一抗对照）

### 2.8 检测系统
- 聚合物法 HRP 标记抗小鼠二抗检测试剂盒（用于 CD68）——如 EnVision+/HRP 或同类产品，即用型
- 聚合物法 HRP 标记抗兔二抗检测试剂盒（用于 MPO）——如 EnVision+/HRP 或同类产品，即用型
- （备选方案）生物素化二抗 + 链霉亲和素-HRP（LSAB/ABC 系统）

### 2.9 显色/复染/封片
- DAB（3,3′-二氨基联苯胺）显色底物试剂盒（显色浓缩液 + 底物缓冲液，现配现用）
- Mayer 苏木精或 Harris 苏木精，即用型——细胞核复染
- 1% 酸性乙醇（1% HCl 溶于 70% 乙醇）——如需分化时使用
- 氨水（0.1–1%）或 Scott 自来水替代液——返蓝试剂
- 梯度乙醇（70%、95%、100%）——脱水
- 二甲苯——透明
- 中性树胶等永久性封片剂及盖玻片

### 2.10 缓冲液/通用耗材
- TBST 或 PBST 洗涤缓冲液，含 0.05% Tween-20（配方见 2.12）
- 湿盒（染色托盘底部铺湿纸巾/纱布，加盖）
- pH 计、计时器、染色缸/染色架、转移吸管

### 2.11 组织对照
- CD68 阳性对照：人扁桃体或淋巴结（生发中心巨噬细胞、窦组织细胞阳性）
- MPO 阳性对照：人扁桃体、脾脏或骨髓（粒细胞阳性，脾脏红髓中染色尤为强）
- MPO 阴性组织对照：结肠（上皮细胞应为阴性；间质/血管内散在粒细胞仍可着色，属正常现象）

### 2.12 缓冲液及试剂配制方法

| 试剂 | 配方 |
|---|---|
| 柠檬酸盐缓冲液，10 mM，pH 6.0（1×） | 二水合柠檬酸三钠 2.94 g 溶于 1 L 蒸馏水；加入 Tween-20 0.5 mL（终浓度 0.05%）；用 1 N HCl 调至 pH 6.0。使用前预热至修复温度。 |
| Tris-EDTA 缓冲液，pH 9.0（1×） | Tris 碱 1.21 g + EDTA 二钠盐 0.37 g 溶于 1 L 蒸馏水；加入 Tween-20 0.5 mL（终浓度 0.05%）；用 NaOH/HCl 调至 pH 9.0。 |
| TBST 洗涤缓冲液（1×） | 0.05 M Tris-HCl，0.15 M NaCl，pH 7.6，加 0.05% Tween-20（可先配 10× Tris 缓冲盐水母液，稀释 1:10 后再加 Tween-20）。 |
| PBS（1×） | 137 mM NaCl，2.7 mM KCl，10 mM Na₂HPO₄，1.8 mM KH₂PO₄，pH 7.4。 |
| H₂O₂ 工作液（过氧化物酶阻断用） | **石蜡切片：** 3% H₂O₂——30% 母液按 1:10 用甲醇稀释（或用蒸馏水/PBS 配成水溶液版本）。**冰冻切片：0.3% H₂O₂**——30% 母液按 1:100 用甲醇或 PBS 稀释。冰冻切片优先选用较低浓度，因为丙酮固定的组织缺乏福尔马林交联保护，在较高过氧化氢浓度下更容易发生氧化性形态损伤及表位丢失；甲醇本身也能加速血红素基团破坏，使较低浓度即可有效淬灭内源性过氧化物酶活性，孵育时间可与高浓度方案相当或更短。现配现用，当次使用后弃去。 |
| 蛋白/血清封闭液 | 10% 正常血清（山羊或马血清——与二抗宿主种属匹配）+ 1% BSA，溶于 TBST 或 PBS。 |
| 抗体稀释液（如不使用商品化稀释液） | 1% BSA + 0.05% Tween-20 溶于 TBS 或 PBS。 |
| DAB 工作液 | 按试剂盒说明将显色浓缩液加入底物缓冲液中现配现用（常见比例为每 1 mL 底物缓冲液加 1 滴，约 20 µL，浓缩液——具体以所用试剂盒说明书为准）。轻柔颠倒混匀，不要剧烈振荡。配好后对光敏感且不稳定，只配当次所需用量。 |
| 返蓝试剂 | 0.1–1% 氨水溶于自来水，或商品化 Scott 自来水替代液（碳酸氢钠/硫酸镁溶液）。 |

以下步骤中的加样体积均按约 1 cm² 大小、以疏水屏障笔圈定的组织区域估算；切片较大时请按比例增加。

## 3. 流程 A — CD68（KP1），石蜡切片（FFPE）

**台面准备：** 二甲苯染色缸 3 个，梯度乙醇染色缸 4 个（100% ×2、95%、70%），流动蒸馏水，预热好的 10 mM pH 6.0 柠檬酸盐缓冲液（盛于耐修复容器中），TBST 洗涤缓冲液，现配 3% H₂O₂，蛋白封闭液，已稀释至工作浓度的一抗，即用型 HRP 聚合物抗小鼠二抗，现配 DAB 工作液，Mayer 苏木精，氨水，脱水/透明系列，封片剂，PAP 笔，湿盒，计时器。

1. **切片：** 石蜡组织块切片，厚度 4 μm；于 45–50 °C 水浴中展平，贴附于荷电载玻片。竖直晾干 10–15 分钟，再平放于 56–60 °C 烤箱中烤片 60 分钟，确保组织贴附牢固。
2. **脱蜡：** 二甲苯缸 1，浸泡 5 分钟 → 二甲苯缸 2（新鲜二甲苯），浸泡 5 分钟。
3. **复水：** 100% 乙醇，2 次更换，每次 5 分钟 → 95% 乙醇，5 分钟 → 70% 乙醇，5 分钟 → 流动蒸馏水冲洗 2 分钟。自此步骤起，切片不得再干燥。
4. **抗原修复：** 将载玻片放入预热好的 10 mM pH 6.0 柠檬酸盐缓冲液中。用蒸锅或水浴箱加热至 95–99 °C，恒温修复 20 分钟（替代方案：高压锅 121 °C 加热约 3 分钟，但仅在该方案已在本实验室单独验证过的前提下使用）。关闭加热，让载玻片在缓冲液内自然冷却至室温，需 20–30 分钟后再取出（骤冷或高温状态下更换缓冲液有导致组织脱片的风险）。
5. **洗涤：** TBST，2 次，每次 5 分钟，轻柔摇动。
6. **画圈：** 用 PAP 笔沿组织边缘外约 2–3 mm 画圈，晾干 1–2 分钟。此举可将后续约 100–150 µL 的试剂用量限制在组织区域内。
7. **过氧化物酶阻断：** 滴加约 100–150 µL 现配 3% H₂O₂，完全覆盖切片。室温孵育 10 分钟，置于加盖染色盒内（避免强光直射）。TBST 洗涤，2 次，每次 5 分钟。
8. **蛋白封闭：** 滴加约 100–150 µL 10% 正常血清（与二抗宿主匹配，如正常山羊血清）+ 1% BSA（溶于 TBST）。室温湿盒内孵育 20–30 分钟。轻轻甩去多余液体，无需冲洗，直接进行下一步一抗孵育。
9. **一抗孵育：** 将 CD68（KP1）一抗用抗体稀释液稀释至 1:50–1:100（起始范围，需结合本地滴定结果确认）。每张切片滴加约 100 µL，确保完全覆盖组织。室温孵育 30–60 分钟，或 4 °C 湿盒过夜。同时按相同流程处理一张同型对照片（小鼠 IgG1，终浓度与一抗一致）和一张不加一抗对照片（仅加稀释液）。
10. **洗涤：** TBST，3 次，每次 5 分钟，每次均需彻底更换缓冲液。
11. **二抗/检测：** 滴加约 100–150 µL 即用型 HRP 聚合物抗小鼠试剂。室温孵育 30 分钟。TBST 洗涤，3 次，每次 5 分钟。
12. **显色：** 现配 DAB 工作液（配方见 2.12）。每张切片滴加约 100 µL。室温显色 5–10 分钟，每隔 2–3 分钟在显微镜下观察显色程度；当棕色信号与背景对比达到理想程度时，立即浸入蒸馏水终止反应（显色过度会增加背景）。
13. **冲洗：** 流动蒸馏水，5 分钟。
14. **复染：** Mayer 苏木精，1–2 分钟。流动自来水冲洗至水色变清（1–2 分钟）。
15. **返蓝：** 氨水（或 Scott 替代液）浸泡 30–60 秒至细胞核变蓝。流动自来水冲洗 1–2 分钟。
16. **脱水：** 70% 乙醇 1 分钟 → 95% 乙醇 1 分钟 → 100% 乙醇，2 次，每次 2 分钟。
17. **透明：** 二甲苯，2 次，每次 2–5 分钟。
18. **封片：** 滴加 1–2 滴永久性封片剂，将盖玻片以小角度缓慢覆盖，避免产生气泡。避光平放固化数小时（过夜可完全固化）后再进行拍照观察。

**预期染色结果：** 巨噬细胞、组织细胞及 Kupffer 细胞样细胞呈胞浆颗粒状阳性。需注意 KP1 克隆因共同表位对成纤维细胞存在已知交叉反应（并非真正的 CD68 表达），判读时应结合形态学。

## 4. 流程 B — CD68（KP1），冰冻切片

**台面准备：** 冷冻切片机（腔体 –18 至 –22 °C / 样本头 –18 至 –20 °C），预冷 100% 丙酮（–20 °C），PBS 或 TBS 洗涤缓冲液，现配 0.3% H₂O₂（仅显色流程需要），蛋白封闭液，一抗，HRP 聚合物抗小鼠二抗（或荧光标记抗小鼠二抗，用于免疫荧光），DAB 工作液或水性封片剂，PAP 笔，湿盒。

1. **冰冻切片：** 将 OCT 包埋的新鲜冰冻组织切片，厚度 5–8 μm；采用贴附法将切片贴于荷电载玻片上（用室温载玻片轻触切片使其融化贴附）。室温晾干 30–60 分钟。若不立即染色，可单独包裹后置于 –80 °C 保存（可稳定数月）；使用前在原包装内于室温复温 20–30 分钟，避免冷凝水直接落在组织上。
2. **固定：** 浸入预冷（–20 °C）100% 丙酮，10 分钟。取出后室温晾干 5–10 分钟（丙酮固定后不要立即用水性溶液冲洗，需待其完全挥发）。
3. **画圈：** 晾干后用 PAP 笔圈定组织区域。
4. **洗涤：** PBS 或 TBS，2 次，每次 5 分钟，轻柔摇动。
5. **过氧化物酶阻断（仅显色流程需要，免疫荧光可省略）：** 滴加约 100–150 µL 0.3% H₂O₂（用甲醇或 PBS 稀释，见 2.12），室温 10 分钟。此处采用比石蜡切片（3%）更低的浓度，是因为丙酮固定的冰冻组织缺乏福尔马林交联保护，在较高过氧化氢浓度下更容易出现氧化性形态损伤及表位丢失。PBS/TBS 洗涤，2 次，每次 5 分钟。
6. **蛋白封闭：** 滴加约 100–150 µL 10% 正常血清（与二抗宿主匹配）+ 1% BSA，室温湿盒内孵育 20–30 分钟。
7. **一抗孵育：** CD68（KP1）起始稀释度 1:50（需本地滴定；冰冻切片最佳稀释度不如石蜡切片规范化）。每张切片滴加约 100 µL。室温孵育 30–60 分钟，或 4 °C 湿盒过夜。同时设同型对照片及不加一抗对照片。
8. **洗涤：** PBS/TBS，3 次，每次 5 分钟。
9. **二抗（显色分支）：** 滴加约 100–150 µL 即用型 HRP 聚合物抗小鼠试剂，室温 30 分钟。洗涤 3 次，每次 5 分钟。
10. **显色（显色分支）：** 按流程 A 第 12 步配制并滴加 DAB；显色 5–10 分钟，显微镜下监控；冲洗。
11. **复染/脱水/透明/封片（显色分支）：** 同流程 A 第 14–18 步。

**免疫荧光分支（如选择 IF 而非 DAB，替换第 9–11 步）：**
9a. 滴加荧光标记抗小鼠二抗，常用稀释度 1:200–1:500，每张约 100 µL。室温避光孵育 30–60 分钟。
10a. PBS 避光洗涤，3 次，每次 5 分钟。
11a. 如需细胞核复染：DAPI，300 nM，避光孵育 5 分钟；短暂冲洗。
12a. 用水性抗淬灭封片剂封片；若非当天拍照，可用透明指甲油封边；避光平放存于 4 °C。

## 5. 流程 C — MPO 多克隆抗体，石蜡切片（FFPE）

**台面准备：** 同流程 A，将二抗替换为 HRP 聚合物抗兔试剂，阴性对照替换为非免疫兔血清/兔 IgG。

1–3. **切片、脱蜡、复水：** 同流程 A 第 1–3 步。
4. **抗原修复：** 柠檬酸盐缓冲液 pH 6.0 或 Tris-EDTA 缓冲液 pH 9.0（厂商资料中均记载对该靶点有效）——95–99 °C，20 分钟；缓冲液中自然冷却 20–30 分钟。（另一经验证的替代方案：pH 7.8 修复液，高压灭菌锅 121 °C 处理 5 分钟——仅在本实验室已验证该组合有效时使用。）
5. **洗涤：** TBST，2 次，每次 5 分钟。
6. **画圈：** PAP 笔圈定，晾干 1–2 分钟。
7. **过氧化物酶阻断：** 滴加约 100–150 µL 3% H₂O₂，室温 10 分钟。TBST 洗涤，2 次，每次 5 分钟。
8. **蛋白封闭：** 滴加约 100–150 µL 10% 正常血清（山羊血清，与抗兔二抗匹配）+ 1% BSA，室温 20–30 分钟。
9. **一抗孵育：** MPO 多克隆抗体稀释至 1:300–1:600（厂商针对石蜡切片给出的起始范围；部分厂家/批次实际有效浓度可能需要稀释到 1:150 左右——务必先用该批次在阳性对照组织上滴定，再用于实验切片）。每张切片滴加约 100 µL。室温孵育 30–60 分钟，或 4 °C 过夜。同时设非免疫兔血清/IgG 对照及不加一抗对照，浓度尽量与一抗匹配。
10. **洗涤：** TBST，3 次，每次 5 分钟。
11. **二抗：** 滴加约 100–150 µL 即用型 HRP 聚合物抗兔试剂，室温 30 分钟。TBST 洗涤，3 次，每次 5 分钟。
12. **显色：** 按流程 A 第 12 步配制并滴加 DAB；显色 5–10 分钟，显微镜下监控。
13–18. **冲洗、复染、返蓝、脱水、透明、封片：** 同流程 A 第 13–18 步。

**预期染色结果：** 中性粒细胞谱系（自早幼粒细胞至分叶核中性粒细胞各阶段）呈弥漫强阳性胞浆/颗粒状染色；单核细胞谱系染色弱且不稳定；淋巴细胞、红系及巨核系前体、间质树突状细胞、肥大细胞、血管内皮细胞预期阴性。用于谱系鉴别时应作为组合套餐的一部分判读，不宜单独作为依据。

## 6. 流程 D — MPO 多克隆抗体，冰冻切片

1–4. **冰冻切片、固定、画圈、洗涤：** 同流程 B 第 1–4 步。
5. **过氧化物酶阻断（仅显色流程需要）：** 滴加约 100–150 µL 0.3% H₂O₂（用甲醇或 PBS 稀释，见 2.12），室温 10 分钟。与流程 B 同理，较低浓度可保护丙酮固定组织免受氧化损伤；对 MPO 而言这一点尤其值得注意，因为 MPO 本身就是一种含血红素的过氧化物酶——这一步实际上是在淬灭组织内源性粒细胞中真实存在的、与目标抗原同类的酶活性，而不只是无关的背景酶。PBS/TBS 洗涤，2 次，每次 5 分钟。
6. **蛋白封闭：** 滴加约 100–150 µL 10% 正常血清（山羊血清，与抗兔二抗匹配）+ 1% BSA，室温 20–30 分钟。
7. **一抗孵育：** MPO 多克隆抗体，起始稀释度 1:150–1:300（相比石蜡切片范围更浓，因缺少 HIER 带来的表位增强效应）——需本地滴定。每张切片滴加约 100 µL。室温孵育 30–60 分钟，或 4 °C 过夜。同时设非免疫兔血清/IgG 及不加一抗对照。
8. **洗涤：** PBS/TBS，3 次，每次 5 分钟。
9. **二抗（显色分支）：** 滴加约 100–150 µL 即用型 HRP 聚合物抗兔试剂，室温 30 分钟。洗涤 3 次，每次 5 分钟。
10. **显色（显色分支）：** DAB，5–10 分钟，显微镜下监控；冲洗。
11. **复染/脱水/透明/封片：** 同流程 A 第 14–18 步。

**免疫荧光分支：** 将第 9–11 步替换为荧光标记抗兔二抗（约 1:200–1:500，室温避光 30–60 分钟）、可选 DAPI（300 nM，5 分钟）及水性抗淬灭封片，操作与流程 B 免疫荧光分支相同。

## 7. 对照设置（适用于以上四种流程）

- **阳性组织对照：** 每次染色批次均需与待测切片同批处理，经历相同的固定/修复条件。
- **阴性试剂对照：** 用稀释液替代一抗，或使用浓度匹配的同型/非免疫对照抗体，在连续切片或相邻切片上进行。
- **内部阴性对照：** 利用切片内本就不表达目标抗原的细胞类型（如结肠上皮相对于 MPO）作为额外的内置核查点。
- 每次染色应记录抗体批号、抗原修复条件及孵育时间；MPO 为多克隆抗体，批次间效价差异明显大于 CD68 单克隆抗体，更换批号时应重新滴定。

## 8. 常见问题排查（简表）

| 问题 | 可能原因 | 调整建议 |
|---|---|---|
| 背景过深 | 封闭不充分；一抗浓度过高；洗涤不足 | 延长血清封闭时间；进一步稀释一抗；增加洗涤次数/时长 |
| 信号弱或缺失 | 抗原修复不足；抗体浓度过低；试剂失效；固定时间超过 48 小时 | 延长修复时间或更换缓冲体系（柠檬酸盐 ↔ Tris-EDTA）；提高抗体浓度；核查试剂有效期 |
| CD68 出现间质/成纤维细胞非特异性着色 | KP1 克隆与巨噬细胞谱系外的共同表位存在已知交叉反应 | 结合形态学判读，必要时联合第二种巨噬细胞标志物（如 CD163）确认 |
| 确认为中性粒细胞的区域 MPO 染色偏弱 | 抗原修复不充分，或该批次抗体效价偏低 | 针对当前批次，用阳性对照（扁桃体/脾脏）重新滴定 |

## 9. 关于证据强度与参数确定性的说明

**已被厂商说明书及文献充分证实的内容：**
- CD68（KP1）识别的是溶酶体/胞浆糖蛋白，标记单核/巨噬细胞谱系，同时有文献明确记载其因共同表位而对成纤维细胞存在非谱系特异性反应性。
- MPO 多克隆抗体标记髓系（粒细胞）谱系各成熟阶段细胞，单核细胞染色弱且不稳定，淋巴系、红系、巨核系及间质/血管细胞为阴性。
- 两种抗体均经厂商验证可用于石蜡切片及冰冻切片 IHC。
- 第 2.12 节给出的整体 IHC 操作顺序及标准缓冲液配方是通用实验室标准方法，与具体所用抗体无关。

**依厂家/批次不同而变化、需本地验证后方可用于诊断或可报告结果的内容：**
- 具体工作稀释度：文献及说明书记载范围差异较大，CD68（KP1）从原厂 Dako 克隆在石蜡切片上的 1:50–1:100，到部分其他厂家高效价/重组制品的 1:3000 不等；MPO 多克隆抗体工作稀释度在文献中大致分布于 1:150 至 1:600 之间，因供应商及批次而异。
- 具体修复缓冲液及时长：柠檬酸盐 pH 6.0 与 Tris-EDTA pH 9.0 均有文献支持对这两个靶点有效，但实际修复效率会因固定时长、检测系统灵敏度不同而有差异。
- 冰冻切片专用的稀释度及孵育参数：相较于石蜡切片（厂商说明书主要基于石蜡包埋扁桃体验证），冰冻切片相关参数在一手资料中记载相对有限；上文给出的冰冻切片起始稀释度属合理起点，并非厂商认证数值。
- 上述加样体积（约 100–150 µL）及封闭/孵育时间遵循的是常见实验室惯例，而非单一的普适标准；请根据切片大小及所用检测试剂盒说明书酌情调整。

鉴于上述参数存在批次及厂家依赖性，本方案应被视为一个已验证的起点，而非可直接照搬的最终参数。按照实验室质控/认证的通用要求，每更换一个新批号抗体、每引入一种新组织类型，均应先针对已知阳性对照进行滴定验证，再将结果用于判读或发表。

**修订说明（冰冻切片过氧化物酶阻断浓度）：** 本文档早前版本在四个流程中统一使用 3% H₂O₂。现已针对冰冻切片流程（B、D）修正为 0.3% H₂O₂——这是文献中针对丙酮固定冰冻组织普遍推荐的浓度，原因是此类组织缺乏福尔马林交联保护，在 3% 浓度下更容易发生氧化性损伤及表位丢失。石蜡切片流程（A、C）仍保留 3% H₂O₂，与经福尔马林交联组织的常规做法一致。

**关于通透化处理（本方案未加入的原因）：** 丙酮固定冰冻切片属于溶剂型固定方法，其固定过程本身就会溶解膜脂质；通用方法学资料普遍将丙酮/醇类固定描述为"固定与通透化同步完成"，与经醛类交联（如 PFA 固定）的组织不同，通常不需要额外的去垢剂通透化步骤。已发表的针对颗粒/溶酶体相关抗原（包括 CD68 和 MPO）的丙酮固定染色方案，大多是固定后直接进入封闭步骤，并未额外加入去垢剂处理。加入非离子型去垢剂（如 Triton X-100）在此情境下并没有明确证据支持其必要性，反而存在一个针对本应用场景的实际代价：MPO 是嗜天青颗粒内的一种可溶性酶，更剧烈的膜破坏可能使颗粒内容物在抗体结合前发生扩散，从而模糊掉用于区分真实中性粒细胞谱系信号与弥漫背景信号的、具有判读意义的颗粒状染色模式——这是一个关乎染色模式判读的考量，而不仅仅是灵敏度问题。基于以上原因，本方案未加入去垢剂步骤；如果在确认抗原修复及稀释度均非限制因素后信号仍异常偏弱，将其作为一个可实证测试的备选方案仍然合理。
